# Internal Communications Skill

Comprehensive guidance for creating professional, effective internal communications across various formats and contexts.

## Core Communication Principles

### 1. Know Your Audience
- Identify primary and secondary audiences
- Understand their information needs
- Adjust technical depth accordingly

### 2. Lead with Impact
- Put most important information first (inverted pyramid)
- Use clear, concise headlines
- Make action items immediately visible

### 3. Be Clear and Actionable
- Use specific, concrete language
- Define clear next steps and owners
- Include deadlines and timelines

### 4. Show Progress with Data
- Use metrics to demonstrate impact
- Visualize data when possible
- Balance quantitative and qualitative information

## Communication Types

### Status Reports
**Standard Structure:**
1. Executive Summary (1-2 sentences)
2. Key Metrics & Progress
3. Accomplishments/Wins
4. Challenges & Blockers
5. Upcoming Priorities
6. Help Needed

### Company Newsletters
**Standard Sections:**
1. From Leadership (optional)
2. Company Updates & Milestones
3. Team Spotlights & Recognition
4. New Hires & Announcements
5. Upcoming Events
6. Learning & Development

### All-Hands Announcements
**Standard Structure:**
1. Clear Subject Line
2. TL;DR Summary (2-3 bullets)
3. Context & Background
4. The Announcement
5. Why This Matters
6. What Happens Next
7. Action Items
8. FAQ Section

### Team Updates
**Standard Structure:**
1. Sprint/Period Summary
2. Wins & Accomplishments
3. Key Metrics
4. Learnings
5. Upcoming Work
6. Shout-outs

## Tone Guidelines

### Professional Yet Approachable
- Use conversational but clear language
- Avoid corporate jargon
- Show personality within bounds

### Action-Oriented
- Use active voice
- Start with verbs
- Make requests specific

## Quick Reference

### Decision Tree
```
Need to communicate something?
├─ Urgent and affects everyone? → All-hands announcement
├─ Regular update on progress? → Status report
├─ Celebrating wins? → Newsletter or recognition
├─ Policy or process change? → Policy announcement
└─ Team-specific progress? → Team update
```

### Formality Spectrum
Most Formal → Least Formal:
Policy changes → All-hands → Status reports → Newsletters → Slack
