# Hashtag Strategy for RedNote English Posts

## The 3-Tier Hashtag System

Every post should use **8–15 hashtags** drawn from three tiers for maximum reach:

| Tier | Size | Purpose | Count per Post |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Niche** | <500K posts | Targeted, less competition, high conversion | 4–5 |
| **Tier 2 — Mid** | 500K–5M posts | Balanced reach and relevance | 3–4 |
| **Tier 3 — Broad** | 5M+ posts | Discovery amplification | 2–3 |

## Hashtag Placement Rules

1. Place hashtags at the **very end** of the post, separated by a line break
2. Format: each hashtag on the same line, space-separated OR grouped by category
3. Do **not** embed hashtags mid-paragraph (disrupts reading flow)
4. Do **not** use periods or commas after hashtags

**Example format:**
```
[Post body ends here]

#skincarecommunity #glasskin #koreanbeautytips #dewyskin #skincarecheck #skincareroutine #beautyreview #skincare #beauty #selfcare #rednote #notsponsored
```
