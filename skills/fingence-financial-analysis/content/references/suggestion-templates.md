# Intelligent Recommendation Templates Reference

This document defines templates and standard formats for recommendations generated by Fingence.

## Recommendation Generation Principles

### 1. Data-Driven Principle
- Each recommendation must be traceable to specific data
- Annotate data source (file, period, account)
- Avoid subjective speculation

### 2. Actionable Principle
- Recommendations must specify Who, What, How
- Avoid vague statements like "strengthen management"
- Quantify expected effects

### 3. Classification Principle
| Urgency | Annotation | Description |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Urgent | 🔴 | Requires immediate action, exceeds threshold or significant risk |
| Important | 🟡 | Requires attention this week, affects business metrics |
| General | ℹ️ | Recommend monitoring, can be included in improvement plan |

### 4. Compliance Principle
- ⚠️ Tax recommendations must include disclaimer
- ⚠️ Recommendations involving regulations must note "Please consult a professional"

## 1. Business Recommendation Templates

### Scenario 1: Revenue Growth Slowdown

**Trigger Condition**: YoY revenue growth rate declines for 2 consecutive periods

**Template**:
```
【Business Recommendation】Revenue Growth Slowdown Alert

📊 Analysis Finding
  Issue: 2024 Q2 revenue YoY growth 5%, significantly lower than Q1's 12%
  Data Support:
    - 2024 Q1 Revenue: 10.5M (YoY +12%)
    - 2024 Q2 Revenue: 11.03M (YoY +5%)
    - Growth rate declined 7 percentage points

🔴 Urgency Level: Important

📋 Actionable Recommendations

  1. 【Customer Dimension Analysis】
     Owner: Sales Director
     Action: Review Q2 lost customer list, analyze churn reasons
     Expected: Identify recovery opportunities, estimated additional revenue 500K/month

  2. 【Product Dimension Optimization】
     Owner: Product Manager
     Action: Compare Q1/Q2 product sales structure, identify slow-moving products
     Expected: Optimize product mix, increase high-margin product share

  3. 【Channel Efficiency Improvement】
     Owner: Operations Manager
     Action: Analyze customer acquisition cost by channel, eliminate low-efficiency channels
     Expected: Reduce CAC 15%, improve ROI

⏰ Recommended Execution Timeline: Complete analysis this week, evaluate effects next month
```

### Scenario 2: Gross Margin Decline

**Trigger Condition**: Gross margin QoQ decline exceeds 3 percentage points

**Template**:
```
【Business Recommendation】Gross Margin Decline Analysis

📊 Analysis Finding
  Issue: June 2024 gross margin 28%, down 4 percentage points from May's 32%
  Data Support:
    - June Revenue: 3.8M
    - June COGS: 2.74M
    - May Gross Margin: 32%
    - June Gross Margin: 28%

🟡 Urgency Level: Important

📋 Possible Causes

  1. Raw material cost increase
     Evidence: June raw material purchase price increased 8%
     Impact: Lowered gross margin approximately 2%

  2. Product mix change
     Evidence: High-margin Product A sales share decreased from 45% to 35%
     Impact: Lowered gross margin approximately 1.5%

  3. Increased promotional discounts
     Evidence: June discounted sales share increased to 20%
     Impact: Lowered gross margin approximately 0.5%

📋 Actionable Recommendations

  1. 【Cost Control】
     - Renegotiate with suppliers, lock in raw material prices
     - Evaluate feasibility of alternative raw materials
     - Expected effect: Cost reduction 3%, improve gross margin 0.8%

  2. 【Product Mix Optimization】
     - Increase promotion efforts for high-margin products
     - Set promotion thresholds (price reduction instead of direct discount)
     - Expected effect: Product mix optimization, improve gross margin 0.5%

  3. 【Scale Effect】
     - Evaluate feasibility of increasing purchase volume for discounts
     - Expected effect: Purchase cost reduction 2%, improve gross margin 0.3%
```

## 2. Cost Control Recommendation Templates

### Scenario 1: Abnormal Expense Growth

**Trigger Condition**: YoY or QoQ growth of specific expense exceeds 30% or single transaction exceeds limit

**Template**:
```
【Cost Control Recommendation】Abnormal Expense Growth Analysis

📊 Analysis Finding
  Issue: Sales Dept 2 Q2 travel expense YoY growth 45%, significantly higher than department revenue growth 15%
  Data Support:
    - Sales Dept 2 Q2 Travel Expense: 420K (Q1: 290K)
    - YoY increase: 130K (+45%) 📈
    - Same period department revenue growth: +15%
    - Travel expense to revenue ratio: 8.4% (same period last year: 6.2%)

🟡 Urgency Level: Important

📋 Root Cause Analysis
  Through sampling 30 reimbursement records:
  1. Customer visit reimbursements account for 60%, but new customer sign-ups only grew 10%
  2. 3 round-trip airfares with suboptimal routing
  3. Some entertainment expenses classified as travel reimbursements

📋 Actionable Recommendations

  1. 【Cost-Effectiveness Monitoring】
     Measure: Establish dashboard linking travel expense to sales performance
     Owner: Financial Analysis
     Timeline: Launch within this week

  2. 【Travel Policy Optimization】
     Measure: Set travel standards (advance booking, optimal routing)
     Owner: Sales Director
     Timeline: Update policy this month

  3. 【Reimbursement Training】
     Measure: Reiterate boundaries between travel and entertainment expenses
     Owner: HR
     Timeline: Training next Monday

💰 Expected Effect
  If properly executed, Q3 travel expense expected to decrease 20%, saving approximately 80K
```

```
  2. IT system upgrade costs not included in annual budget
  3. Natural growth in personnel costs

📋 Actionable Recommendations

  1. 【Immediate Hemostasis】

     - Apply for additional budget 500K (requires CFO approval)
     - Or reallocate budget from other departments to admin
     - Owner: Finance Manager

  3. 【Long-term Optimization】

     - Consolidate low-utilization systems
     - Expected savings: 300K/year

⏰ Action Timeline
  This week: Freeze non-essential spending
  This month: Complete budget adjustment application
  Next quarter: Evaluate office space optimization plan
```

**Trigger Condition**: Projected cash outflow in next 30 days > cash balance + projected inflow

**Template**:
```

  Projected 30-Day Inflow: 4.2M
  Projected 30-Day Outflow: 6.2M
  ─────────────────────────────
  Projected Ending Balance: 3.8M
  ⚠️ Projected Gap: 1.2M
  Expected Occurrence Date: 2024-07-15

🔴 Urgency Level: Urgent

📋 Funding Gap Sources
  1. Supplier Payments: 3.5M (payment terms due)
  2. Employee Salaries: 1.5M (July 15)
  3. Tax Payment: 800K (quarterly filing)
  4. Other Expenses: 400K

📋 Response Plans

  【Plan A】AR Collection (Priority: High)
  - Collectible Amount: 1.5M
  - Key Customers:
    · Customer A: 800K (30 days overdue)
    · Customer B: 500K (due this week)
    · Customer C: 200K (due next week)
  - Expected Collection: 1.0-1.2M
  - Owner: Sales Director
  - Timeline: Initiate this week

  【Plan B】Adjust Payment Schedule (Priority: Medium)
  - Postpone non-urgent supplier payments: 500K
  - Negotiate extended payment terms: 10-15 days
  - Expected Relief: 500K
  - Note: Requires maintaining supplier relationships

  【Plan C】Financing (Priority: Low)
  - Activate bank credit line: 1M
  - Cost: 4.35% annual rate
  - Applicable: If above plans are insufficient

💡 Recommended Execution Combination
  Prioritize Plan A + Plan B, expected to cover gap
  Reserve Plan C as backup

⚠️ Disclaimer
  This projection is based on current data, actual results may vary due to business changes
```

### Scenario 2: AR Aging Deterioration

**Trigger Condition**: AR aging exceeding 90 days accounts for more than 20%

**Template**:
```
【Cash Flow Warning】AR Aging Deterioration

📊 Analysis Finding
  Issue: Current AR total 3.2M, of which aging exceeding 90 days accounts for 28%, collection risk exists
  Data Support:
    - Total AR: 3.2M
    - Aging Distribution:
      · Within 30 days: 1.8M (56%)
      · 31-60 days: 500K (16%)
      · 61-90 days: 350K (11%)
      · Over 90 days: 550K (17%) ⚠️
    - Actually exceeding 90 days (including overdue): 900K (28%) 🔴

🟡 Urgency Level: Important

📋 High-Risk Customer List
  1. Company A: 350K (60 days overdue, customer financial difficulty)
  2. Company B: 250K (45 days overdue, in dispute)
  3. Company C: 200K (90+ days overdue, expected difficult to collect)

📋 Actionable Recommendations

  1. 【Tiered Collection】
     - 30-60 days: Automatically send reconciliation notices
     - 60-90 days: Phone follow-up + visit
     - 90+ days: Send legal notice, initiate legal proceedings
     - Owner: Finance + Legal

  2. 【Customer Classification】
     - Suspend new orders for high-risk customers
     - Require prepayment or guarantee
     - Owner: Sales Director

  3. 【Bad Debt Provision】
     - Recommend provisioning 50% bad debt for 90+ days AR
     - Full provision for overdue one year+
     - Impact: Estimated profit reduction 450K
     - Owner: Finance Manager

💰 Expected Effect
  If collection successful, 600-800K can be collected
  Avoid bad debt loss 300K+
```

### Scenario 1: Input Tax Anomaly

**Template**:
```
【Tax Risk Warning】Input Tax Deduction Anomaly

📊 Analysis Finding
  Issue: 2024 Q2 input tax structure abnormal, toll charges proportion too high
  Data Support:
    - Total Input Tax: 850K
    - Including: Toll charges 280K (33%) ⚠️
    - Toll charges proportion far exceeds industry average (5-10%)

⚠️ Risk Level: Medium

📋 Possible Risks
  1. Toll charge deductions may not comply with regulations (need to comply with Caishui [2016] No. 47)
  2. Risk of duplicate or false deductions
  3. May trigger tax inspection

📋 Rectification Recommendations

  1. 【Self-Inspection】
     - Verify each toll invoice against actual business
     - Confirm invoice title, date, and amount consistency
     - Owner: Finance Specialist
     - Timeline: This week

  2. 【Standardize Management】
     - Establish toll expense reimbursement standards
     - Each reimbursement requires attached itinerary description
     - Owner: Finance Manager

  3. 【Professional Consultation】
     - Recommend contacting tax advisor to confirm deduction compliance
     - If risk exists, proactively file corrected returns

⚠️ Disclaimer
  This notice is for reference only and does not constitute tax advice.
  Please refer to the latest policies of the State Taxation Administration and your local tax authority.
  If you have questions, it is recommended to consult a professional tax advisor or your local tax authority.
```

## Recommendation Quality Checklist

After generating recommendations, self-check the following:

- [ ] Does each recommendation have clear data support?
- [ ] Does the recommendation specify the owner (Who)?
- [ ] Does the recommendation have actionable steps (What)?
- [ ] Does the recommendation quantify expected effects?
- [ ] Do tax recommendations include disclaimer?
- [ ] Are recommendations classified by urgency level?
- [ ] Do recommendations have timeline?
