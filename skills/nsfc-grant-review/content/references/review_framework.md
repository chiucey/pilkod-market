# NSFC Grant Review Framework

## Review Dimensions

Every NSFC project review covers 5 standardized dimensions. Generate review comments
for each dimension from the perspective of 7 simulated expert reviewers.

### Dimension 1: Innovation (研究选题的创新性)

Evaluate:
- Is the research question genuinely novel?
- Does the proposal introduce new concepts, theories, or methodologies?
- Has the core idea already been published by the applicant?
- Is the scope appropriate?

Common critique angles:
- Core hypothesis already published (创新性略显不足)
- Novel concept insufficiently validated
- Research direction unclear
- Scope too broad

### Dimension 2: Scientific Value (科学价值)

Evaluate:
- Importance of the scientific problem
- Potential to advance the field
- Clinical/translational significance
- Whether findings could open new directions

Common critique angles:
- Scientific significance overstated
- Connection between structure/function and disease unclear
- Findings may not generalize

### Dimension 3: Feasibility (研究方案的可行性)

Evaluate:
- Logic and coherence of research design
- Appropriateness of methods and techniques
- Adequacy of controls
- Clarity of technical details
- Risk mitigation

Common critique angles:
- Experimental logic not clear
- Inappropriate cell/animal models
- Lack of essential controls
- Over-reliance on a single technique
- Overexpression systems where endogenous models needed
- Methods too limited

### Dimension 4: Team & Preliminary Work (研究队伍与前期基础)

Evaluate:
- Qualifications and expertise of PI and team
- Quality and relevance of preliminary data
- Applicant's track record
- Availability of equipment and facilities

Common critique angles:
- Preliminary data weak or not directly relevant
- PI's recent output insufficient
- Team lacks specific expertise
- Key preliminary experiments missing

### Dimension 5: Other Suggestions (其他建议)

Provide constructive suggestions or write "无" if none:
- Technical improvements
- Additional experiments or controls
- Methodological innovations
- Any other concerns

## 7 Reviewer Profiles

### Reviewer <1>: Supportive Generalist
- Generally positive assessment
- Acknowledges proposal strengths
- Minor, gentle concerns
- Sets a constructive tone

### Reviewer <2>: Critical Methodologist
- Balanced but focuses on experimental issues
- Questions cell line choices, controls, technical details
- References specific methods from the proposal
- Mixed overall assessment

### Reviewer <3>: Rigorous Cell Biologist
- Detailed technical critiques
- Focuses on experimental design flaws
- Questions specificity of staining, co-localization claims
- References specific proteins and pathways

### Reviewer <4>: Field Expert
- Evaluates novelty relative to published work
- Knows the applicant's prior publications
- Assesses whether work extends or duplicates published findings
- Often critical about innovation

### Reviewer <5>: Moderate Evaluator
- Fair, balanced assessment
- Acknowledges both strengths and weaknesses equally
- Assesses practical feasibility and significance
- Thoughtful, measured comments

### Reviewer <6>: Harsh Skeptic
- Highly critical, emphasizes weaknesses
- Questions innovation, team strength, feasibility
- May raise doubts about research conditions or prior output
- Strong negative comments on multiple dimensions

### Reviewer <7>: Constructive Expert
- Supportive overall but with specific suggestions
- Offers concrete technical improvements
- Suggests alternative models or approaches
- Balanced with a constructive orientation

## Tone and Language Guidelines

### Language
- Primary: Chinese (formal academic)
- Technical terms: English (gene names, protein names, cell lines)
- Standard scientific terminology

### Tone
- Objective and professional
- Specific to proposal content
- Constructive when critical
- Concise: 1-3 sentences per dimension

### Sentence Pattern Examples

**Positive (innovation):**
- "该项目选题具有一定的创新性，申请人前期发现了[具体发现]，为[领域]提供了新的研究视角。"
- "本项目从[独特角度]研究[科学问题]，具有较高的原创性。"

**Negative (innovation):**
- "本项目的核心观点和部分研究内容已在[期刊]发表，研究选题的创新性略显不足。"
- "机制设计思路不清晰，研究方向不明，生理意义不明，整个研究选题缺乏创新性。"

**Positive (feasibility):**
- "研究方案具有较好可行性，研究层次较为清晰。"
- "实验设计基本合理可行。"

**Negative (feasibility):**
- "研究方案逻辑不够顺畅，对于各部分研究内容没有有机的整合起来，关联性不够清晰。"
- "实验设计缺乏基本的对照。"
- "研究方案大量使用过表达体系，建议设计knockin的小鼠品系以及人源化模型去研究。"

**Positive (team):**
- "研究队伍构成较合理。申请人长期[领域]研究，有多项成果和积累。"

**Negative (team):**
- "申请人近5年没有参与或主持的基金，没有高水平的相关研究文章。"
- "研究队伍结构也不合理，直接支持本项目的前期研究结果不足。"

## Content Fidelity Rules

1. **Always reference actual proposal elements** — never invent genes, proteins,
   cell lines, methods, or publications
2. **Use exact technical terms** from the proposal (e.g., if proposal says
   "albumosome", do not substitute "白蛋白聚集体" unless the proposal uses both)
3. **Cite actual journals** mentioned in the proposal, never substitute
4. **Reference actual experimental systems** (specific cell lines, animal models,
   techniques described in the proposal)
5. **Keep comments concise** — 1-3 sentences captures the reference style
6. **Be specific, not generic** — "HEK293T as expression cell is not suitable
   for studying liver protein interactions" is better than "the cell line choice
   is inappropriate"

## Discipline-Specific Focus Areas

### Biology/Medical Proposals
- Cell/animal model appropriateness
- Control adequacy
- Antibody/staining specificity
- In vivo vs in vitro relevance

### Chemistry/Materials Proposals
- Characterization methodology
- Scalability and application potential
- Comparison with existing methods

### Engineering/CS Proposals
- Technical novelty vs existing solutions
- Experimental validation
- Real-world applicability

### Interdisciplinary Proposals
- Integration logic
- Team complementarity
- Interdisciplinary justification
