# ACS Journal Paper Format Guidelines

## Basic Requirements

### Page Setup
- Page size: Letter (8.5 × 11 inches)
- Margins: 1 inch top/bottom, 1 inch left/right
- Double spacing
- Font: Times New Roman, 12 pt

### Title
- All capital letters
- Concise and clear, reflecting core research
- Typically no more than 12 words

### Author Information
- Full name, affiliation, address
- Corresponding author marked with *

### Abstract
- Standalone paragraph
- 200-300 words
- Structured abstract: Purpose + Methods + Results + Conclusions
- No abbreviations or citations

### Keywords
- 5 or more
- Separated by semicolons
- Avoid words from the abstract

## Text Formatting

### Headings
1. Bold, title case
2. Bold, title case
3. Italic, title case

### Tables and Figures
- Each should have a caption and legend
- Table/Figure + number
- Image resolution ≥ 300 DPI
- Three-line table format for tables

### References
- Numbered sequentially
- Journal abbreviations (refer to CAS standards)
- Include DOI

## Tense Usage Guidelines

| Section | Tense | Description |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| Introduction | Present | Describe known facts, existing problems |
| Methods | Past | Describe experimental operations |
| Results | Past/Present | Use past for observations, present for figures |
| Discussion | Present | Interpret and analyze, cite literature |

## Common Expression Templates

### Research Background
- "X-based materials have attracted considerable attention..."
- "Recently, significant progress has been made in..."
- "Despite advances in..., challenges remain..."

### Research Objective
- "In this work, we demonstrate..."
- "Herein, we report..."
- "We present a facile strategy for..."

### Experimental
- "The synthesis was carried out by..."
- "The samples were characterized using..."
- "Control experiments were performed to..."

### Results
- "As shown in Figure 1,..."
- "The diffraction peaks at 2θ = ...° correspond to..."
- "A significant enhancement in ... was observed..."

### Conclusions
- "In summary, we have developed..."
- "These findings provide insights into..."
- "The approach demonstrated here offers a promising route to..."

## Supporting Information

- Numbered corresponding to the main text
- Contains additional experimental data
- Consistent formatting with the main text
- Submitted as a separate file
