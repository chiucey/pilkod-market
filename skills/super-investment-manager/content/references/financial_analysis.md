# Financial Statement Analysis Template

## Core Financial Metric System

### I. Profitability Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Excellent | Monitor | Warning |
|--------|---------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Gross Margin | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100% | >40% | 20%-40% | <20% |
| Net Margin | Net Profit / Revenue × 100% | >20% | 10%-20% | <10% |
| ROE | Net Profit / Equity × 100% | >20% | 10%-20% | <10% |
| ROA | Net Profit / Total Assets × 100% | >10% | 5%-10% | <5% |
| EPS | Net Profit / Shares Outstanding | Continuous growth | Flat | Declining |

### II. Growth Capability Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Excellent | Monitor | Warning |
|--------|---------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Revenue Growth | (Current - Prior) / Prior × 100% | >30% | 10%-30% | <10% |
| Net Profit Growth | (Current - Prior) / Prior × 100% | >30% | 10%-30% | <10% |
| Non-GAAP Growth | (Current - Prior) / Prior × 100% | >30% | 10%-30% | <10% |
| 3-Year CAGR | (End / Start)^(1/3) - 1 | >25% | 15%-25% | <15% |

### III. Financial Health Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Excellent | Monitor | Warning |
|--------|---------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Debt-to-Asset | Total Debt / Total Assets × 100% | <50% | 50%-70% | >70% |
| Current Ratio | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | >2 | 1-2 | <1 |
| Quick Ratio | (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liab. | >1 | 0.5-1 | <0.5 |
| Cash/Net Profit | Operating Cash Flow / Net Profit | >1 | 0.5-1 | <0.5 |

### IV. Valuation Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Excellent | Monitor | Warning |
|--------|---------|-----------|---------|---------|
| PE (TTM) | Price / EPS | <20x | 20-40x | >40x |
| PB | Price / Book Value Per Share | <3x | 3-5x | >5x |
| PS | Price / Revenue Per Share | <5x | 5-10x | >10x |
| PEG | PE / Profit Growth Rate | <1 | 1-2 | >2 |

## Financial Statement Analysis Checklist

### Balance Sheet Analysis
- [ ] Total asset scale and trend
- [ ] Liability structure (current/non-current)
- [ ] Debt-to-asset ratio changes
- [ ] Receivables turnover
- [ ] Inventory turnover
- [ ] Goodwill and impairment risk
- [ ] Interest-bearing debt level

### Income Statement Analysis
- [ ] Revenue scale and growth rate
- [ ] Gross margin and trend
- [ ] Expense ratio (Sales/G&A/Finance)
- [ ] Net profit and growth
- [ ] Non-GAAP net profit quality
- [ ] Non-recurring items percentage

### Cash Flow Statement Analysis
- [ ] Operating cash flow
- [ ] Operating cash flow / Net profit ratio
- [ ] Investing cash flow direction
- [ ] Financing cash flow changes
- [ ] Free cash flow situation

### DuPont Analysis
```
ROE = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
    = (Net Profit / Revenue) × (Revenue / Total Assets) × (Total Assets / Equity)
```

**ROE Improvement Paths**:
1. Improve net margin (product upgrade, expense control)
2. Improve asset turnover (efficiency improvement)
3. Moderate leverage (financial leverage)

## Financial Warning Signals

### Red Warning (Immediate Attention)
1. Revenue/Net profit declining for 2+ consecutive quarters
2. Operating cash flow negative for 2+ consecutive years
3. Debt-to-asset ratio > 80%
4. Receivables growing significantly with slower turnover
5. Goodwill > 30% of total assets
6. Major shareholders frequently selling
7. Management team frequently changing

### Yellow Warning (Continue Monitoring)
1. Gross margin continuously declining
2. Expense ratio abnormally rising
3. Inventory significantly growing
4. Customer concentration increasing
5. Related party transactions increasing
