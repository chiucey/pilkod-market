# Discovery and decision record

## First question group

Ask no more than three prompts at once:

1. What topics, industries, companies, products, communities, or event types should the briefing track or exclude?
2. How often should it arrive, at what local time, and in which timezone? Clarify weekday-only and quiet-day behavior.
3. Should delivery stay inside MiniMax as a recurring briefing, or become a website?

## Editorial preferences

Resolve item count, reading time, content types, domestic/international balance, languages, preferred and blocked sources, translation, recency, low-news-day behavior, and manual versus unattended publication.

Never fill a quota with weak or duplicate stories. Offer fewer items or a labeled evergreen item when current coverage is insufficient.

## Website-only questions

Ask about the existing repository/site/host, public versus private access, visual references and brand assets, required modules, localization, and browser-local versus cross-device saves. If no visual reference exists, take design ownership and state the proposed direction in the plan.

## Confirm with a compact specification

Summarize topics, exclusions, item count, source/language mix, cadence, time, timezone, delivery form, approval boundary, and storage model. Ask the user to correct only unresolved or incorrect fields.
