# Rights Proposal Competition Full Process Guide

## Competition Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Competition Name | 10th Rights Proposal Competition, BIT Teli Division |
| Team Size | Within 6 people, individual or team participation allowed |
| Submission | Email: bit-xtltw-bgs@qq.com |
| Deadline | March 27, 18:00 |
| Advancement | Top 25 proposals advance to school-level semi-finals |

## Proposal Topic Directions

### Four Major Themes

1. **Education & Teaching**
   - Subject major construction, curriculum setup & optimization
   - Teaching management system, teacher teaching quality
   - Teaching infrastructure, exam & certification management
   - Academic research support

2. **Growth & Development**
   - Academic ability cultivation, employment & innovation guidance
   - Student organization construction, reward & punishment mechanisms
   - Campus culture construction, international exchange & development

3. **Life Services**
   - Student accommodation, campus dining
   - Sports facilities & equipment, medical & health services
   - Campus service facilities (supermarket, express delivery station, etc.)
   - Campus network & information services

4. **Rights Protection**
   - Campus environment safety & governance
   - Mental health counseling & services
   - Campus order management
   - Student feedback channel optimization

## Proposal Format Requirements

Standard proposals must include the following four parts:

### 1. Proposal Background
Briefly describe the current situation and importance of the issue.

### 2. Detailed Proposal Description
- List specific problems in detail
- Show preliminary research results
- Provide survey data support (questionnaires, observations, etc.)

### 3. Suggested Feasible Solutions
- Present solutions hierarchically with key points highlighted
- Propose specific actionable solutions
- Explain implementation conditions and feasibility

### 4. Proposal Significance
- Explain the proposal's prospects
- Describe advantages and expected effects
- Quantify the benefiting population

## Disqualification Criteria

Proposals will not be accepted if they:
- Involve state secrets or prohibited content
- Address personal matters for self or relatives
- Already handled by the school or beyond school authority
- Are vague without specific suggestions
- Are plagiarized or highly similar to existing proposals
- Violate the one-issue-per-proposal principle

## Scoring Criteria

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Issue Importance | 25% | Scope of affected people, relevance to campus life |
| Research Depth | 25% | Data support sufficiency, problem analysis accuracy |
| Solution Feasibility | 30% | Solution specificity, actionability |
| Proposal Standards | 20% | Format compliance, content completeness, clarity |

## Submission Requirements

1. **File Format**: Word document (.docx)
2. **Naming**: Use "Proposal Title" as filename
   - Example: `Proposal on Improving Lunchtime Crowding at North Canteen.docx`
3. **Content Structure**: Proposal collection form + specific proposal (in same document)
4. **Proposal Summary**: Required, used for online voting

## Excellent Proposal Characteristics

Reference 2025 winning proposals, they share these features:
- **Authentic Issues**: From real research, not subjective speculation
- **Data Support**: Questionnaire/interview/field survey data
- **Specific Solutions**: Clear, actionable recommendations
- **Standard Format**: Complete structure, professional language
- **Clear Significance**: Value and expected effects explained
