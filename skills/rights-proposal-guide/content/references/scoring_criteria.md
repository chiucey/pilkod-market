# Rights Proposal Competition Scoring Guidelines

## Scoring Dimensions Overview

| Dimension | Weight | Score |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Issue Importance | 25% | 25 points |
| Research Depth | 25% | 25 points |
| Solution Feasibility | 30% | 30 points |
| Proposal Standards | 20% | 20 points |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100 points** |

---

## I. Issue Importance (25 points)

### Scoring Standards

| Grade | Score | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| Excellent | 21-25 | Issues affect a wide range of people (multiple groups or school-wide), closely related to campus life, representing common concerns |
| Good | 16-20 | Issues affect a certain scope, relatively related to campus life, fairly common |
| Average | 11-15 | Issues affect limited people but related to some students' lives |
| Below Average | 6-10 | Issues are relatively individual, not widespread |
| Poor | 0-5 | Issues are unrepresentative, not common shared requests |

### Evaluation Points
- Audience scope and breadth of impact
- Degree of relevance to campus life
- Urgency and necessity of the issue
- Whether it represents common wishes of students

---

## II. Research Depth (25 points)

### Scoring Standards

| Grade | Score | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| Excellent | 21-25 | Scientific and diverse research methods, sufficient data support (questionnaire/interview/measurement data), accurate and thorough problem analysis with first-hand information |
| Good | 16-20 | Relatively complete research methods with some data support, fairly accurate problem analysis |
| Average | 11-15 | Simple research or data analysis exists but not sufficient |
| Below Average | 6-10 | Lacks data support, mainly subjective descriptions |
| Poor | 0-5 | No research support, purely subjective speculation |

### Evaluation Points
- Diversity of research methods (questionnaires, interviews, field observations, data collection, etc.)
- Sufficiency of sample size
- Authenticity and reliability of data
- Accuracy and depth of problem analysis

---

## III. Solution Feasibility (30 points)

### Scoring Standards

| Grade | Score | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| Excellent | 26-30 | Solutions are specific and clear with strong actionability; implementation conditions clear and budget reasonable; effects can be anticipated and evaluated |
| Good | 21-25 | Solutions are relatively specific with strong actionability; has some implementation condition explanations |
| Average | 16-20 | Solutions have direction but not specific enough, average actionability |
| Below Average | 11-15 | Solutions are vague, lacking actionability |
| Poor | 0-10 | No specific solutions or completely infeasible |

### Evaluation Points
- Specificity and clarity of suggestions
- Hierarchy and logic of solutions
- Feasibility of implementation conditions
- Reasonableness of budget costs
- Evaluability of expected effects

---

## IV. Proposal Standards (20 points)

### Scoring Standards

| Grade | Score | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| Excellent | 18-20 | Compliant format and complete structure (includes four parts), clear structure, professional and fluent language, accurate and concise proposal summary |
| Good | 14-17 | Relatively compliant format, clear structure, good language expression |
| Average | 10-13 | Basic format meets requirements but some content missing or average expression |
| Below Average | 6-9 | Non-compliant format, incomplete content, confused expression |
| Poor | 0-5 | Completely does not meet format requirements |

### Evaluation Points
- Whether includes all four parts: background, detailed description, solutions, significance
- Completeness and logic of structure
- Professionalism and fluency of language
- Accuracy and conciseness of proposal summary

---

## Bonus Points

The following may receive bonus points (up to 5 points):

| Bonus Item | Score | Description |
|------------|-------|-------------|
| Multi-disciplinary perspective | +1-2 | Analyzes issues from multiple perspectives |
| Innovative solutions | +1-2 | Proposes novel and unique solutions |
| Cross-regional comparative research | +1 | References practices from other universities |

---

## Deduction Points

The following will result in deductions:

| Deduction Item | Score | Description |
|----------------|-------|-------------|
| Plagiarism/High similarity | Disqualification | |
| Severely non-compliant format | -5 points | Missing core sections |
| Empty proposal summary | -3 points | Not filled as required |
| Late submission | -5 points | Submitted after deadline |

---

## Review Process

### Preliminary Round (Teli Division)
1. **Online voting** (30% weight)
   - All students can participate
   - Score based on ranking

2. **Judge review** (70% weight)
   - Review panel composed of student union and youth league committee teachers
   - Scored according to these guidelines

### Semi-Finals Review
1. **Review committee review** → Enter online voting
2. **Online voting** → Top 8-10 by votes advance directly to finals
3. **Department evaluation** → Top 25% enter finals

### Finals Review
1. **Proposal presentation and defense** (within 6 minutes)
2. **On-site judge Q&A**
3. **Comprehensive scoring determines awards**

---

## Common Deduction Reasons Summary

1. Missing or too brief proposal summary
2. Problem analysis lacks data support
3. Solutions too general, lacking specific measures
4. Not written in four-part format
5. Insufficient significance explanation
6. Language not professional or standard
