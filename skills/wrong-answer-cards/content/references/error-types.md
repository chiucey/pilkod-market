# Error Type Classification Reference

## Three Categories Explained

### 1. Careless

**Typical Characteristics:**
- Calculation steps are correct, method is correct, but result is wrong
- Misread numbers, symbols, or conditions
- Missed selections/entries
- Copying errors
- Careless reading of question

**Signal Keywords:**
- "I knew how to do it"
- "I calculated correctly but wrote the wrong answer"
- "I just needed to read the question more carefully"

**Examples:**
- Question: 12×12=? Answer: 144 (wrongly written as 124)
- Question asks for "incorrect option" but selected "correct option"

---

### 2. Vague Knowledge

**Typical Characteristics:**
- Knows general direction but details are inaccurate
- Confuses similar but different concepts
- Formulas/theorems are partially remembered
- Understanding is incomplete, seems uncertain

**Signal Keywords:**
- "I thought it was like this"
- "I remember there's a formula but forgot the exact one"
- "I've seen this topic but not very sure"

**Examples:**
- Confusing power functions with exponential functions
- Remembering formula incorrectly (e.g., remembering (a+b)² as a²+b²)

---

### 3. Completely Unfamiliar

**Typical Characteristics:**
- Completely doesn't understand the question
- Doesn't know the related topic
- No idea where to start
- Random guessing

**Signal Keywords:**
- "Completely don't understand"
- "Haven't learned this concept"
- "Can't figure out where to start"

**Examples:**
- Question involves unlearned knowledge
- Can't understand what the question is asking at all
