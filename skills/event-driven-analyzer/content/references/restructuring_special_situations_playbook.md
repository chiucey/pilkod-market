# Restructuring and Special Situations Playbook

Reference for dated public special-situation events around distressed securities, exchange offers, liability management, bankruptcy process milestones, index events, de-SPACs, rights offerings, warrants, preferreds, and technical situations. Organized **by event archetype**, because special-situations work shares a common skeleton but the specific catalyst type determines what to underwrite first.

## Boundary statement

This skill owns the dated equity-side event path: probability tree, timing, payoff bridge, expected return, listed-equity expression, and monitoring. 独立信用分析流程 owns capital structure mapping, covenant / document review, liquidity, maturity wall, priority, fulcrum-security analysis, and recovery waterfalls. When those credit outputs drive the answer and are not already supplied, route the credit work to 独立信用分析流程 and use the results as terminal-payoff inputs.

## Default objective

Identify which dated restructuring, exchange, court, maturity, or liability-management catalyst is the next price-mover before the market re-prices, and quantify the scenario payoffs and probabilities around that catalyst.

## Required fact pack (applies to every archetype)

- Issuer, tickers, securities.
- Dated catalyst: exchange deadline, forbearance date, maturity, coupon date, RSA milestone, court hearing, vote, plan confirmation, rights offering, index / technical date, or lockup / float event.
- Current security price, timestamp, liquidity, borrow, expected holding period.
- Existing terminal payoff or recovery assumptions, labeled by source.
- Debt prices, yields, spreads, CDS where available.
- Equity market cap and enterprise value.
- Exchange offer / LME / RSA / bankruptcy status.
- Court dates, creditor groups, DIP, plan, disclosure statement where applicable.
- Inputs to source from 独立信用分析流程 when needed: capital structure, maturity wall, liquidity runway, covenant headroom, collateral, guarantees, structural subordination, intercreditor terms, priority, value break, fulcrum security, recovery values, plan valuation.

## Default workflow

1. Decide whether the task is an event-path question or a credit / recovery question; route credit / recovery work to 独立信用分析流程.
2. Identify the dated catalyst: maturity, covenant test, interest payment, lawsuit, exchange deadline, RSA, court hearing, vote, confirmation, or technical event.
3. Convert supplied credit / recovery assumptions into event terminal payoffs.
4. Build scenario probabilities, timing, expected value, downside.
5. Treat legal / process risks (priming, uptier, drop-down, fraudulent transfer, class vote, cramdown, appeal) as event-path risks rather than as legal conclusions.
6. Recommend listed-equity or event expression only when payoff inputs are sourced or properly caveated; route debt-security expression to 独立信用分析流程.
7. Build monitoring plan and kill criteria.

## Senior judgment questions

- Is the equity a real option, or just retail optionality?
- If a fulcrum security is central, has 独立信用分析流程 supplied the liquidity and ownership view?
- Can the company execute an out-of-court exchange?
- Who holds a blocking position or de facto control?
- Is priming risk or collateral leakage a credible scenario?
- Does the recovery depend on business value or on litigation leverage?
- Is the price compensating for process complexity and time?

## Archetype A — Index and technical flow events

Use when the catalyst is forced buying / selling, index inclusion / deletion, rebalance, lockup expiry, flow event, convert technical, warrant exercise, rights offering, or float change.

Analyze:
- Event date and mechanics.
- Who must buy or sell.
- Estimated flow vs. average daily value traded.
- Float and liquidity.
- Crowding and pre-positioning.
- Borrow and options.
- Fundamental anchor after the flow.
- Exit plan.

Senior questions:
- Is the flow already priced?
- Is there enough liquidity to monetize the dislocation?
- Does the trade have a fundamental floor once technical pressure ends?
- Is the flow one-day, multi-day, or persistent?

---

## Archetype B — De-SPAC / SPAC situations

Analyze:
- Trust value.
- Redemption deadline and expected redemption rate.
- PIPE size and conditions.
- Minimum cash condition.
- Sponsor promote and earnout.
- Warrant dilution.
- Lockups.
- Float and borrow.
- Projections quality.
- Post-close liquidity and financing need.

Senior questions:
- Is the target viable without projected growth?
- Does redemption break the minimum cash condition?
- Is float so small that price action becomes technical rather than fundamental?
- Are warrants or converts a cleaner expression than common?

---

## Archetype C — Rights offerings and capital raises

Analyze:
- Subscription price.
- Oversubscription rights.
- Backstop party and fees.
- Use of proceeds.
- Dilution.
- Shareholder participation incentives.
- Trading value of rights.
- Balance-sheet impact.

Senior questions:
- Is the capital raise value-creating, or is it a rescue financing?
- Is the backstop extracting economics from non-participating shareholders?
- Are rights mispriced relative to common?

---

## Archetype D — Preferreds, warrants, converts, and stubs

Analyze:
- Conversion / exercise terms.
- Call / put features.
- Dividends / coupons and arrears.
- Change-of-control provisions.
- Liquidity and borrow.
- Path to monetization.
- Control-party incentives.

---

## Output requirements (all archetypes)

- Security expression and any required handoff to 独立信用分析流程.
- Event / catalyst timeline.
- Scenario tree.
- Recovery, SOTP, or payoff value only where sourced, supplied, or incorporated from 独立信用分析流程.
- Best expression and why.
- Liquidity / crowding / borrow assessment.
- Monitoring plan.
- Red-team critique.

## 独立信用分析流程 boundary, restated

Distressed exchange, bankruptcy, covenant, recovery, and debt-security selection route to 独立信用分析流程. The equity-side event view in this skill may use 独立信用分析流程 outputs only as inputs to a listed-equity event analysis.
