# Output Templates

Use these templates to keep feedback actionable.

## Editorial Verdict

```markdown
**Editorial Verdict**
Likely outcome: [Desk reject / Send to review but high major-revision risk / Major revision / Minor revision / Near-ready]
Target lane: [journal or venue class]
Readiness grade: [A-E]

Why:
1. [Main reason]
2. [Main reason]
3. [Main reason]

What would move it up one level:
- [Concrete action]
- [Concrete action]
```

## Top-Risk Table

```markdown
| Priority | Severity | Location | Diagnosis | Why it matters | Concrete fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Critical | Abstract/Intro | [problem] | [editor/reviewer risk] | [revision action] |
```

Severity:
- Critical: likely desk rejection or fatal review concern.
- Major: likely major revision.
- Moderate: weakens argument but fixable.
- Minor: polish or formatting.

## Response Style

Be direct but useful:
- Say "This is a desk-reject risk because..." when true.
- Distinguish "must fix" from "would strengthen."
- Give replacement structure or wording when diagnosing a problem.
- Avoid vague comments like "improve clarity" unless paired with a concrete operation.
