# Movie Resource Search Guide

## Search Strategy

### 1. Keyword Optimization
- Chinese movies: Full title + year
- English movies: English title + year
- Series: Add episode/season number

### 2. Search Variants
```
Basic: {title}
Enhanced: {title} 1080P
Enhanced: {title} BluRay
Enhanced: {title} 4K
Enhanced: {title} HDR
```

## FAQ

### Q: Why is download speed slow?
A: Magnet links depend on seeders. Choose resources with more seeders.

### Q: How to get subtitles?
A: Search "{movie} subtitles" and download SRT/ASS format subtitle files.

### Q: Cannot play after download?
A: Install appropriate codec. Recommend PotPlayer or VLC player.
