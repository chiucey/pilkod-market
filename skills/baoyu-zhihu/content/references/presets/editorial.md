# EDITORIAL 风格预设

## Deco
大引号装饰、左侧竖线引用、书写感

## Font
衬线体或楷体、温暖色调

## Feel
人文感、故事性、个人观点

## Color
#2C2C2C #8B7355 #D4A853 #F5E6C8

## Prompt
Zhihu editorial style, warm white background, serif typography, large quote marks decoration, narrative storytelling aesthetic, personal and warm tone
