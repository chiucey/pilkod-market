# Academic Writing Style Guide

## Default Style Rules

### 1. Voice and Tone
- **Formal academic tone** required
- **Passive voice** preferred over active
- **NO first-person** pronouns: I, we, our, us
- **NO second-person**: you, your

### 2. Prohibited AI Phrases
Do NOT use these phrases (AI over-formal):
- leverage
- delve into
- facilitate
- navigate
- landscape
- crucial
- pivotal
- testament
- harness
- cutting-edge
- groundbreaking
- state-of-the-art
- it is worth noting
- it is important to note
- as mentioned earlier
- Furthermore

### 3. Sentence Structure
- Maximum **30 words** per sentence
- Maximum **2 levels** of nested clauses
- Avoid run-on sentences
- One idea per sentence

### 4. Paragraph Structure
- **Topic sentence first** - states the main argument
- Supporting evidence follows
- Clear logical flow
- One core argument per paragraph
- Minimum 3 sentences per paragraph

### 5. Terminology
- Use **glossary-consistent** terms throughout
- Match case exactly (e.g., "machine learning" not "Machine Learning")
- Define abbreviations at first use
- Use consistent plural forms

### 6. Citations
- Default: **APA 7th Edition**
- In-text: (Author, Year)
- Reference list at end
- DO NOT use footnote citations

### 7. Numbers
- Spell out numbers one to nine
- Use numerals for 10 and above
- Exception: measurements, statistics, equations

## Citation Examples (APA 7th)

### Journal Article
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. *Journal Name, Volume*(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxxx

### Book
Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work: Capital letter also for subtitle*. Publisher.

### Conference Paper
Author, A. A. (Year, Month). Title of paper. *Conference Name*, Location. URL

## Section Templates

### Introduction
1. Broad context (1-2 paragraphs)
2. Gap in literature (1 paragraph)
3. Research objective (1 paragraph)
4. Main contributions (1 paragraph)

### Abstract (Structured)
1. Background (1-2 sentences)
2. Objective (1 sentence)
3. Methods (2-3 sentences)
4. Results (2-3 sentences)
5. Conclusion (1-2 sentences)

### Literature Review
1. Thematic organization
2. Synthesis not summary
3. Identify gaps
4. Position your work

### Methodology
1. Research design
2. Participants/subjects
3. Procedures
4. Data analysis
5. Limitations

### Results
1. Descriptive statistics
2. Inferential statistics
3. Tables and figures
4. Non-redundant presentation

### Discussion
1. Summary of findings
2. Comparison with literature
3. Implications
4. Limitations
5. Future research

## Word Count Guidelines

| Section | Approximate % |
|---------|---------------|
| Abstract | 5% |
| Introduction | 20-25% |
| Literature Review | 20-25% |
| Methodology | 15-20% |
| Results | 15-20% |
| Discussion | 20-25% |
