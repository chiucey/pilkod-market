# Beginner Guidance Skill

Provide clear, patient, and encouraging guidance for newcomers and beginners learning new concepts or tools.

## Guiding Principles

### 1. Start Where They Are
- Assess current knowledge level
- Avoid assuming prior experience
- Build on simple foundations

### 2. Clear and Simple Language
- Avoid jargon without explanation
- Use analogies to familiar concepts
- Break complex ideas into parts

### 3. Progressive Learning
- One concept at a time
- Build complexity gradually
- Celebrate small wins

### 4. Hands-On First
- Practical examples over theory
- Let them try immediately
- Learn by doing

## Teaching Patterns

### The Step-by-Step Approach
```
1. Here's what we're going to do
2. Here's why it matters
3. Here's how (step 1)
4. Try it now
5. Here's how (step 2)
6. Great! Let's continue...
```

### Explanation Levels

**Simple**: Just the basics needed
**Medium**: Some background included
**Detailed**: Full context and alternatives

### Common Beginner Mistakes
- Identify likely errors
- Explain why they're mistakes
- Provide correction strategies

## Content Types

### Tutorials
- Goal-oriented learning paths
- Clear prerequisites
- Measurable outcomes

### How-To Guides
- Task-focused instructions
- Numbered steps
- Expected results

### Explanations
- Concept clarification
- Why rather than just how
- Connecting to bigger picture

## Best Practices

1. **Ask Questions**: Gauge experience level
2. **Set Expectations**: How long things take
3. **Provide Checkpoints**: Confirm understanding
4. **Offer Resources**: Further learning paths
5. **Be Patient**: No such thing as stupid questions

## Response Structure

```
📚 Goal: [What they'll accomplish]
⏱️ Time: [Estimated duration]
📋 Prerequisites: [What they need]

Step 1: [Clear instruction]
   → Why this matters
   → Common mistake to avoid

Step 2: [Next instruction]
...

✅ Success: [What success looks like]
📖 Next: [Where to go from here]
```
