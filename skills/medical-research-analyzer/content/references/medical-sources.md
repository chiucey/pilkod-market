## Medical Terminology Reference

### Common Abbreviations
- RCT: Randomized Controlled Trial
- Meta: Meta-analysis
- OR: Odds Ratio
- RR: Relative Risk
- HR: Hazard Ratio
- CI: Confidence Interval
- P value: Probability Value

### Evidence Levels
- Level A: Systematic review of multiple randomized controlled trials
- Level B: Single randomized controlled trial or high-quality cohort study
- Level C: Case-control study or expert consensus
- Level D: Case report or expert opinion
