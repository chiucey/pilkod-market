# Quantum Stock God - A-Share 20-Dimensional Quantitative Analysis System

## Core Analysis Framework

### 20-Dimensional Analysis System

This skill provides a systematic stock analysis framework to help investors evaluate stock investment value and risk from multiple perspectives. **Important Notice: This analysis tool is for reference only and does not constitute specific investment advice. Investment decisions are your sole responsibility.**

#### I. Capital & Institution Dimensions (1-5)

1. **Main Capital Net Inflow/Outflow**
   - Definition: Single transactions exceeding 1 million RMB
   - Analysis Method: Observe consecutive multi-day inflow trends
   - Interpretation: Sustained inflows may indicate institutional accumulation

2. **Super Large, Large, Medium, Small Order Capital Flow**
   - Super Large Order: Greater than 5 million
   - Large Order: 1-5 million
   - Key Points: Distinguish between active and passive buying ratio

3. **Dragon/Tiger List Data Tracking**
   - Trading seat analysis
   - Famous hot money operation style identification
   - Institutional seats vs. trading seat comparison

4. **Shareholder Count Changes**
   - Decreasing shareholders typically means concentrated chips
   - Focus on quarterly/annual report data changes

5. **Fund Position Changes**
   - Active fund addition/reduction situation
   - Star fund manager position trends

#### II. Volume & Price Technical Dimensions (6-10)

6. **Volume-Price Relationship Analysis**
   - Price up with volume / price up with shrinking volume / price up with volume stagnation
   - Volume-price divergence warning
   - Key position volume changes

7. **Moving Average System Analysis**
   - 5/10/20/60/120/250-day moving averages
   - Golden cross and death cross
   - MA support and resistance levels

8. **MACD Indicator**
   - Golden cross/death cross signals
   - Divergence analysis
   - Red/green bar contraction and expansion

9. **KDJ Stochastic Indicator**
   - Overbought/oversold zones (>80 overbought, <20 oversold)
   - J-value top/bottom warning
   - KDJ golden cross/death cross

10. **Bollinger Bands (BOLL) Analysis**
    - Upper rail resistance, middle rail support, lower rail support
    - Opening and closing analysis
    - Stock price touching Bollinger band edges

#### III. Trend & Pattern Dimensions (11-15)

11. **Trend Line Analysis**
    - Upward channel/downward channel
    - Support and resistance lines
    - Trend line breakout confirmation

12. **Candlestick Pattern Recognition**
    - Bottom patterns: Double bottom, triple bottom, head and shoulders bottom
    - Top patterns: Double top, triple top, head and shoulders top
    - Continuation patterns: Triangle, flag, wedge

13. **Volume Pattern**
    - Cup and handle formation
    - Flag consolidation with decreasing volume
    - Volume confirmation at breakout

14. **Wave Theory Application**
    - 5-wave upward structure
    - Retracement ratio analysis (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%)
    - Extended wave identification

15. **Chan Theory Structure**
    - Lines, segments, pivots
    - Trend type judgment
    - Divergence and reversal

#### IV. Fundamentals & Valuation Dimensions (16-18)

16. **Financial Indicator Analysis**
    - Revenue growth rate
    - Net profit growth rate
    - Gross margin trend
    - Return on Equity (ROE)

17. **Valuation System**
    - PE (Price-to-Earnings) ratio
    - PB (Price-to-Book) ratio
    - PEG growth indicator
    - Industry average comparison

18. **Industry Position & Track**
    - Industry market share
    - Core competitiveness analysis
    - Policy impact assessment

#### V. Market Sentiment & Risk Dimensions (19-20)

19. **Market Sentiment Indicators**
    - Number of limit up/limit down stocks
    - Next-day premium rate of limit up stocks
    - Overall market volume changes
    - Margin financing balance

20. **Risk Assessment System**
    - Systematic market risk
    - Individual stock black swan risk
    - Liquidity risk
    - Valuation bubble risk
