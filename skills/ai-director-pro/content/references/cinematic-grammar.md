# Cinematic Visual Grammar

> **Author**: 起始终下
> **Copyright**: © 2025 起始终下. All Rights Reserved.

---

## 1. Write Unresolvable State First

Before entering composition, write clearly in one sentence: which two forces cannot be satisfied simultaneously, what action the character is executing or refusing, what consequence remains after scene ends.

Linear skeleton can be written as:

`Existing event/rules → Character enters or faces space → Executes/refuses action → Produces visible consequence or unresolved decision`

Each key visual must have a story verb: waiting, blocking, handing over, withdrawing, crossing boundary, identifying, deleting, bracing against, giving up. Scenes with only emotional adjectives without action and consequence are not valid.

---

## 2. Relationship Pressure Cards

Before camera positioning, fill:

- Who is looking at whom, who deliberately not looking
- Who knows more, who is misjudging
- Who is preparing to leave, wait, block, or expose
- How doors, windows, tables, mirrors, stairs, corridors, floor lines limit action
- Where audience is placed: participant, bystander, voyeur, observed, blind spot
- Whether person or space dominates, whether relationship changes at end of shot

Composition must serve at least one pressure. Symmetry, whitespace, frame-within-frame or close-ups without relationship pressure are merely decoration.

---

## 3. Gaze Flow Sentence

For each key frame or shot group, write a gaze flow:

`入口 → 阻挡/加速 → 决定性落点 → 尚未解决的边缘信息 → 离开画面的余味`

Example structure: Audience first attracted by door gap light, gaze blocked by foreground shoulder, lands on clenched fist under table, finally sees person not yet entering dialogue in mirror.

If one sentence cannot describe viewing route, the visual is likely overstuffed. Gaze flow allows interruption, but interruption must create misjudgment, late information, or spatial pressure.

---

## 4. Realistic Camera Position Records

Important shots must record feasible camera positions:

- Camera area and shooting axis
- Height, character distance, subject proportion in frame
- Focal length or angle tendency and spatial consequences
- Foreground blocking, focus layer, eye height
- What foreground/midground/background carry separately
- Main light source from which physical fact
- Movement start point, end point, and stopping reason

Cannot only write "cinematic, oppressive, epic, dreamy." When focal length, height, and movement conflict, prioritize relationship pressure and spatial executability.

---

## 5. Visual Engines

Each project or sequence selects one primary engine, maximum two auxiliary engines:

- **秩序 (Order)**: Axis, repetition, symmetry, geometric rules established and broken
- **光 (Light)**: Visible light source changes identity, information, or action permissions
- **色 (Color)**: Color source migrates, attenuates, or reverses in plot
- **空间 (Space)**: Doors, levels, blocking, distance, paths create narrative
- **人物状态 (Character State)**: Posture, breathing, action completeness, performance distance carry change
- **尺度/世界 (Scale/World)**: Character-environment ratio reveals system, natural, collective power
- **社会调度 (Social Blocking)**: Multi-person positioning, furniture, entrances-exits form power geometry
- **主观扰动 (Subjective Disturbance)**: Focus, exposure, motion, sound-image offset represents limited cognition

Primary engine owns composition and editing decisions; auxiliary engines only supplement and cannot compete for all shots simultaneously. Delete when more than three engines in one scheme.

---

## 6. Color Sources

Find colors first from facts: clothing, walls, weather, practical lights, glass/water/plant reflections, period materials, stains from workflow. Prohibited: first apply global filter then fill reason.

Suggest recording per scene:

- Two primary color domains
- One transition color domain
- One accent color ~5-15% of frame
- Physical source and plot function of each color
- Continuity method with previous/next scenes: stable, indoor-outdoor transition, accent migration, gradual fade, or motivated reversal

Accent color appears only when audience needs to see it; do not default to neon, cyan-orange, or high saturation as "cinematic."

---

## 7. Capture Medium & Single Imperfection Family

One continuous visual group selects one capture base: clean digital cinematography, 35mm release print feel, 16mm transfer, MiniDV/early digital, broadcast monitor re-shoot, telephoto compression, or motivated wide-angle witness. Selection must explain how it changes viewing distance, time sense, or information credibility.

Same visual group selects maximum one primary imperfection family: dry dust/scratches, rain and wet reflections, low-output smoke, fluorescent dirty feel, broadcast noise, condensation, sun fade/peeling, etc. Do not stack film grain, flares, chromatic aberration, noise, smoke, and water droplets simultaneously.

Per-shot density budget: 2-3 scene details, 1 primary action, 1 secondary clue, 1 main light source, 1 primary composition mechanism. Delete per narrative priority when exceeding budget.

---

## 8. Controlled Variation & Anti-template Shot Groups

Compare at least three viewing routes internally, then select most story-aligned and recently least-used. Between candidates, change relationship position, audience position, spatial entry, or information landing point—not swap adjective sets.

Shot groups are not fixed as "wide-medium-close" or default to establishing shot endings. Optional structures:

- Space → Person → Evidence
- Order → Anomaly → Consequence
- Observer → Observed → Blind spot
- Distance → Sudden approach → Relationship repositioning
- Pre-event → Threshold action → Irreversible result
- Parallel character fragments → Same action convergence

Third key visual can be: body recovery, group reaction, relationship position change, site continuing operation, rule brief rewrite, or changed action. Empty chairs, dropped objects, lonely props only when plot genuinely needs them.

Proof shot group must differ in at least four fields: shot size, camera height, person-environment ratio, composition mechanism, information density, focus layer, light direction, character state.

---

## 9. Quality Gates

Check for failure evidence:

- Can explain unresolvable state, character action, consequence
- Composition originates from relationship pressure
- Gaze flow has entry point, landing point, unresolved information
- Camera position feasible in real space
- Colors have physical source
- Capture base and imperfection family restrained and consistent
- Shot group avoids fixed shot size ladder and fixed ending shots
- Reference preserves only one primary dimension
- Prompt obeys density budget
- Visual contains story verb, not only atmosphere

When any key item cannot provide textual evidence, return to relationship pressure card or gaze flow and redo—not continue adding style words.

## 10. Forbidden Terms

**Chinese empty words**: 令人叹为观止、令人惊叹、视觉盛宴、完美呈现、震撼人心、美轮美奂、无与伦比、惊艳、绝美

**English empty words**: breathtaking, stunning, captivating, mesmerizing, cinematic masterpiece, visual feast, seamlessly, flawlessly, cutting-edge, next-level

**Psychological words in prompts**: 感到、意识到、内心、仿佛、似乎、悲伤、恐惧、愤怒 (unless as diagnostic label)
