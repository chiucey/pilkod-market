# Story Structure Template

## Three-Act Structure Template

### Act One: Setup (25% of the book)

#### Chapter 1: World Presentation
- **Goal**: Establish the stage for the story
- **Elements**:
  - Era and background introduction
  - Social environment depiction
  - Main characters introduced
  - Daily life shown

#### Chapter 2: Character Building
- **Goal**: Let readers understand the protagonist
- **Elements**:
  - Protagonist's appearance and personality
  - Protagonist's daily life
  - Protagonist's dreams and desires
  - Protagonist's current dilemma

#### Chapter 3: Catalyst
- **Goal**: Break the balance
- **Elements**:
  - Inciting incident occurs
  - Protagonist faces challenges
  - Protagonist must make a choice
  - Story officially begins

#### Chapters 4-7: Deepening
- **Goal**: Establish story rules
- **Elements**:
  - Detailed worldbuilding revealed
  - Other major characters introduced
  - Protagonist begins to act
  - Act One climax (irreversible change)

### Act Two: Confrontation (50% of the book)

#### Development Stage 1: New World
- **Goal**: Protagonist enters new environment
- **Elements**:
  - Protagonist tries to solve problems
  - Meets new allies
  - Learns new skills
  - First success

#### Midpoint Turn
- **Goal**: Story direction changes
- **Elements**:
  - Major discovery or revelation
  - Protagonist gains new resources or abilities
  - Goal escalates
  - False victory or false failure

#### Development Stage 2: Greater Challenges
- **Goal**: Things get progressively worse
- **Elements**:
  - Antagonist intensifies counterattack
  - Allies betray or leave
  - Protagonist suffers major setback
  - All hope seems lost

#### Act Two Climax
- **Goal**: Protagonist hits rock bottom
- **Elements**:
  - Major failure
  - Losing someone important
  - Facing despair
  - Preparing for change

### Act Three: Resolution (25% of the book)

#### Act Three Beginning: Awakening
- **Goal**: Protagonist finds new direction
- **Elements**:
  - Learning from failure
  - Re-examining the problem
  - Gaining key insight
  - Formulating final plan

#### Climax Preparation
- **Goal**: Set up for final confrontation
- **Elements**:
  - Rallying allies
  - Acquiring necessary resources
  - Developing strategy
  - Emotional preparation

#### Final Climax
- **Goal**: Resolve core conflict
- **Elements**:
  - Direct confrontation with antagonist
  - Protagonist's critical choice
  - Greatest sacrifice or gain
  - Ultimate presentation of core theme

#### Resolution
- **Goal**: Show the changed world
- **Elements**:
  - Establishment of new order
  - Characters' new states
  - Final theme elevation
  - Emotional satisfaction for readers
