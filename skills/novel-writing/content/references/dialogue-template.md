# Dialogue Scene Templates

## Scene 1: First Meeting

```
【Setting】
Location: Train station/cafe/street
Time: Daytime
Characters: Protagonist A, antagonist/key character B

【Dialogue Design】
A: (Inner) Is this the person?
B: ...
A: Excuse me, is...
B: (Turning around) Oh, it's you.
A: Yes, I'm...
B: (Interrupting) I know who you are. Let's find somewhere to talk.

【Key Points】
- First impression established
- Leave suspense
- Hint at future relationship
- Tight pacing
```

## Scene 2: Intimate Conversation

```
【Setting】
Location: Home/private space
Time: Late night or alone
Characters: Protagonist A, close character B

【Dialogue Design】
A: (Softly) How have you been these years?
B: (Silence) ...Not bad, I guess.
A: (Getting closer) I don't believe it.
B: (Sighing) ...Honestly, sometimes I think of you.
A: (Smiling) Me too.

【Key Points】
- Genuine emotion
- Subtle hints
- Art of leaving space
- Temperature control
```

## Scene 3: Heated Argument

```
【Setting】
Location: Office/home/public place
Time: Emotional breaking point
Characters: Both sides of conflict A, B

【Dialogue Design】
A: You don't understand at all!
B: (Cold smile) You don't understand? Really?
A: (Raising voice) Don't change the subject!
B: (Getting closer) I think you're the one changing the subject.
A: (Stepping back) ...You've changed.
B: (Pausing) ...You changed first.

【Key Points】
- Emotion escalates layer by layer
- Action paired with dialogue
- Rich subtext
- Conflict root revealed
```

## Scene 4: Key Revelation

```
【Setting】
Location: Secret place/critical moment
Time: Story turning point
Characters: Knowledge holder A, revealed party B

【Dialogue Design】
A: (Taking deep breath) There's something I've never told you.
B: (Alert) What?
A: (Staring directly) About that night...
B: (Nervous) ...Go on.
A: (Heavy) Actually, I...
B: (Holding breath)
A: (Slowly) I was... a witness.

【Key Points】
- Pacing control
- Suspense maintained
- Emotional tension
- Information control
```

## Scene 5: Farewell Scene

```
【Setting】
Location: Airport/station/doorway
Time: Moment of parting
Characters: Two people separating

【Dialogue Design】
A: (Handing luggage) Remember to call me when you arrive.
B: (Taking it) Mm.
A: (Hesitant to speak) ...Take care.
B: (Turning, walking a few steps, then stopping) ...
A: (Watching the retreating figure)
B: (Looking back, softly) Goodbye.
A: (Smiling) ...Goodbye.

【Key Points】
- Restrained emotion
- Touching details
- Leaving space
- Lingering resonance
```

## Dialogue Writing Techniques

### Subtext Design
- Meaning beyond words
- Starting to speak then stopping
- Misunderstanding vs. truth
- Concealment vs. revelation

### Speaking Rhythm
- Short sentences for fast rhythm
- Long sentences for slow rhythm
- Power of silence
- Significance of interruption

### Body Language
- Eye contact
- Hand movements
- Physical distance
- Expression changes

### Scene Atmosphere
- Environment matching
- Weather resonance
- Light usage
- Sense of space
