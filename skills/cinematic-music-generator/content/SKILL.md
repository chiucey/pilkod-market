# Cinematic Music Generator

Generate professional-quality cinematic music tracks from text descriptions. This skill creates movie-style soundtracks, orchestral scores, ambient soundscapes, and custom background music with support for various instruments and sound effects.

## Core Capabilities

- **Cinematic Orchestral Scores**: Epic orchestral arrangements with full symphony ensemble
- **Ambient Soundscapes**: Atmospheric textures for scenes, backgrounds, and transitions
- **Electronic Soundtracks**: Modern synth-based cinematic music
- **Hybrid Orchestral**: Combined orchestral and electronic elements
- **Custom Instrument Combinations**: Specify any instruments or sound effects needed

## Supported Instruments & Sound Categories

### Orchestral Instruments
- Strings: Violin, Cello, Viola, Double Bass, Harp
- Brass: Trumpet, French Horn, Trombone, Tuba
- Woodwinds: Flute, Oboe, Clarinet, Bassoon, Piccolo
- Percussion: Timpani, Snare, Cymbals, Tambourine, Gong

### Electronic & Modern
- Synthesizers, Pads, Arpeggiators
- Bass Drops, Risers, Impacts
- Electronic Drums, Loops

### World & Specialty
- Piano, Guitar (Acoustic/Electric)
- Ethnic Instruments, Choirs
- Ambient Textures, Nature Sounds

## Usage Guidelines

### Basic Usage
Provide a detailed text description of the music you want to create. Include:
- **Mood/Emotion**: Epic, tense, romantic, mysterious, triumphant, melancholic
- **Setting/Context**: Battle scene, love scene, chase sequence, calm moment
- **Tempo/Pace**: Fast, slow, building, steady
- **Instruments**: Any specific instruments you want featured

### Advanced Parameters
- **Duration**: Target length of the music (up to 30 minutes per track)
- **Style**: Orchestral, electronic, ambient, hybrid
- **Intensity**: Low, medium, high, building
- **Structure**: Intro, verse, chorus, bridge, outro

## Output Format

Generated music is delivered in **MP3 format** (128kbps - 320kbps quality) ready for:
- Video backgrounds
- Game soundtracks
- Podcast intros/outros
- Presentation scores
- Film/short movie scores

## Technical Notes

- Maximum duration: 30 minutes per track
- Output format: MP3
- Sample rate: 32000 Hz
- Bitrate: 128kbps (default), 256kbps available
- Generation time: Typically 3-10 minutes per track
- Multiple tracks can be generated in parallel (up to 5)

## Error Handling

If generation fails:
1. Check prompt complexity - simplify if too long
2. Verify instrument names are commonly recognized
3. Try alternative mood/tempo descriptions
4. Reduce duration if timing out

## Quality Tips

For best results:
- Be specific about the mood and setting
- List primary instruments in order of importance
- Include reference to similar music styles if known
- Mention any tempo or dynamic preferences
- Describe the emotional arc if applicable
