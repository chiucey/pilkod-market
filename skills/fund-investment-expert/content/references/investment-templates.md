2. Complete identity verification
3. Link your bank card

1. Search fund codes in the app
2. Click "Systematic Investment"
3. Set monthly amount and date
4. Confirm bank authorization

- Rebalance once a year

## Investment Calculators

### Future Value Formula
```
FV = P × [(1 + r)^n - 1] / r
```
Where:
- FV = Future Value
- P = Monthly Payment
- r = Monthly Return Rate
- n = Number of Months

### Required Investment Calculation
```
P = FV × r / [(1 + r)^n - 1]
```

### Profit-Taking Threshold
| Current Drawdown | Suggested Profit-Taking |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| +5% to +10% | Take 30% profits |
| +10% to +15% | Take 50% profits |
| +15% to +20% | Take 70% profits |
| +20%+ | Take all profits or keep partial |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Panic selling**: Don't sell when market drops
2. **Timing the market**: DCA already handles this
3. **Over-concentration**: Diversify across fund types
4. **Ignoring fees**: Consider management fees
5. **No emergency fund**: Keep 3-6 months expenses first
6. **Chasing hot funds**: Past performance ≠ future returns
