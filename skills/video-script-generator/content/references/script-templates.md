**Input**: "Tomato Egg Stir-fry, 30s, Tutorial, Xiaohongshu"

**Output**:
```

## Overview
- Topic: Zero-fail Tomato Egg Stir-fry
- Style: Tutorial
- Platform: Xiaohongshu
- Total Duration: 30 seconds

---

## Storyboard

| Scene | Duration | Visual | Dialogue | Music/SFX | Camera Tips |
|-------|----------|--------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | 0-5s | Morning kitchen, ingredients on counter | "宝子们！教你们零失败番茄炒蛋～" | Upbeat ukulele | Eye-level, slight zoom in |
| 2 | 5-12s | Hands cutting tomato, cracking eggs | "选红番茄，三个鸡蛋，加点盐" | Knife sounds | Top-down, steady hands |
| 3 | 12-20s | Frying eggs, adding tomatoes | "先炒鸡蛋，再炒番茄出汁" | Sizzle sounds | Side angle, steam visible |
| 4 | 20-30s | Plating, blogger smiling, eating | "做好啦！配米饭绝了～" | Cheerful music | Close-up face, then food shot |

## Key Points
- Keep cuts quick (2-3 seconds each)
- Show final dish prominently
- Add text overlays: "3步搞定" "零失败"
```

## Scene Transition Guide

| Transition | Best For | How to Execute |
|------------|----------|---------------|
| Cut | Fast-paced content | Direct switch |
| Fade | Emotional moments | 0.5s fade in/out |
| Zoom | Emphasis | Slight zoom in/out |
| Swipe | Navigation | Slide hand gesture |
| Jump cut | Energy | Slight position change |

## Camera Movement Cheat Sheet

| Movement | Effect | When to Use |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Static | Focus on content | Tutorials, explanations |
| Pan | Show space | Room tours, reveals |
| Tilt | Show height | Products, food |
| Zoom | Emphasis | Key moments |
| Dolly | Cinematic feel | Vlogs, reviews |
| Shaky | Energy, chaos | Comedy, action |
