## AI & Machine Learning

### Claude (Anthropic)
```markdown
## Overview
Warm, approachable AI assistant with editorial aesthetic. Clean layouts with terracotta accent providing humanistic touch.

## Colors
- **Primary**: #CC785C (Warm Terracotta)
- **Secondary**: #C4A484 (Warm Sand)
- **Background**: #FDFBF7 (Warm White)
- **Text**: #1A1A1A (Near Black)
- **Accent**: #E8864A (Vibrant Orange)

## Typography
- **Font**: Inter (clean, humanist)
- **Weights**: 400 (body), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold)
- **Scale**: 14-16px body, 24-32px headlines

## Elevation
Flat design with subtle border contrasts. No shadows.

## Components
- Buttons: Rounded corners (6px), outlined primary style
- Cards: Minimal borders, warm background tints
- Inputs: Clean lines, generous padding (12px)
```
