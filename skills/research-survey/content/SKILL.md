# Research Survey Skill

## Capability Overview

Research Survey is a comprehensive academic research assistant designed to help scholars, students, and professionals conduct thorough and systematic literature reviews and research investigations.

### Core Capabilities

**1. Academic Search & Discovery**
- Perform multi-database academic searches (Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, arXiv, PubMed, IEEE, ACM, Springer, etc.)
- Intelligent keyword extraction and query optimization
- Citation network exploration
- Related paper discovery

**2. Literature Extraction & Analysis**
- Extract key information from academic papers (abstract, methodology, findings, conclusions)
- Identify research gaps and opportunities
- Compare methodologies across studies
- Track research evolution and development trends

**3. Trend Analysis & Visualization**
- Analyze publication trends over time
- Identify emerging research topics
- Map research frontiers and hot spots
- Generate visual representations of research landscapes

**4. Citation Management**
- Generate properly formatted citations (APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, etc.)
- Build reference bibliographies
- Track citation counts and impact metrics
- Export to reference managers (BibTeX, EndNote, Mendeley)

**5. Research Framework Construction**
- Synthesize findings from multiple sources
- Identify theoretical foundations
- Map conceptual frameworks
- Develop research questions and hypotheses

### Supported Search Languages

- English (primary)
- Chinese (中文)
- Japanese (日本語)
- German (Deutsch)
- French (Français)
- Spanish (Español)
- And other major languages

### Output Formats

- **Structured research summaries** — 系统化研究摘要，不是散乱的搜索结果罗列
- **Annotated bibliographies** — 带注释的参考文献列表
- **Comparative analysis tables** — 对比分析表（方法/指标/规模等多维度）
- **Research trend reports** — 研究趋势报告（含时间线与热点图谱）
- **Benchmark evaluation reports** — 基准评测调研报告（含评估指标对比）
- **Citation metrics** — 引文指标与影响力分析
- **Export-ready reference lists** — 可直接导出的参考文献（APA / MLA / IEEE / Chicago）

### Use Cases

1. **Literature Review**: Comprehensive survey of existing research on a topic
2. **Research Background**: Understanding the state of the art in a field
3. **Gap Analysis**: Identifying unexplored areas and opportunities
4. **Trend Monitoring**: Tracking developments in a research domain
5. **Citation Research**: Finding highly cited and influential papers
6. **Methodology Review**: Comparing approaches across studies
7. **Conference/Grant Preparation**: Gathering relevant background literature

### Search Strategy

The skill employs a systematic six-step approach:

1. **Query Decomposition**: Decompose the user query to better understand what topics are related
2. **Broad Exploration**: Initial wide-ranging search to map the research landscape
3. **Focused Investigation**: Targeted search on specific aspects or subtopics
4. **Depth Enhancement**: Follow citations and references for deeper coverage
5. **Citation-aware scoring**: When there are too many papers related, first focus on those with higher citations
6. **Validation**: Cross-reference findings across multiple sources
7. **Synthesis**: Integrate and organize findings into coherent outputs

### Quality Indicators

- Source credibility assessment
- Publication venue ranking
- Citation impact metrics
- Recency and relevance scoring
- Methodology quality evaluation

### Best Practices

- Start with broad queries before narrowing focus
- Use specific keywords combined with Boolean operators
- Check multiple databases for comprehensive coverage
- Review both recent and seminal/classic papers
- Examine citation networks to find influential works
- Go deeper to get more information
- Build a mindmap or knowledge graph on what you've found
- Track search queries and databases used for reproducibility
