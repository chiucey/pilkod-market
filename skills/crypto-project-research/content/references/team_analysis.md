# Team & Governance Analysis Framework

## Core Team Evaluation

### Background Verification
1. **Identity Verification**
   - LinkedIn profiles
   - Previous company affiliations
   - Academic credentials

2. **Relevant Experience**
   - Blockchain/Web3 experience
   - Relevant domain expertise
   - Leadership track record

3. **Team Composition**
   - Technical vs Business balance
   - Geographic diversity
   - Size adequacy for roadmap

### Key Person Risk
- Single founder dependency
- Critical role concentration
- Succession planning

## Governance Models

### Centralization Spectrum

| Model | Decision Speed | Decentralization | Examples |
|-------|---------------|------------------|----------|
| Fully Centralized | Fast | None | Corporate chains |
| DAO Light | Moderate | Partial | Many L2s |
| Full DAO | Slow | High | Ethereum, Cosmos |

### Governance Mechanisms
1. **On-chain Voting**
   - Token-weighted
   - Quadratic voting
   - Conviction voting

2. **Off-chain Signaling**
   - Discourse forums
   - Temperature checks
   - Informal consensus

3. **Multi-sig Controls**
   - Timelock wallets
   - Hardware security
   - Geographic distribution

## Investor Assessment

### VC Quality Indicators
- **Track Record**: Previous successful investments
- **Value Add**: Network, resources, introductions
- **Reputation**: Market standing
- **Investment Thesis**: Alignment with project

### Notable Backers (Examples)
- a16z: DeFi, Infrastructure focus
- Paradigm: Technical depth
- Multicoin: Thesis-driven
- Delphi: Research-intensive

## Treasury Management

### Transparency Indicators
- Public reporting
- Multi-sig controls
- Regular audits
- Clear spending guidelines

### Diversification
- Stablecoin reserves
- Token buybacks
- Liquidity provision

## Red Flags
- Anonymous team without track record
- Plagiarized whitepaper
- History of failed projects
- Key member departures
- Undisclosed investor terms
