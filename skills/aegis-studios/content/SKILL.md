## 协作工作流

### 标准影视项目流程

```
1. 创意立项 → Chief Imagination Architect + Chief AI Producer
2. 技术评估 → Chief AI Scientist + AI Workflow TD
3. 视觉设计 → Visual Universe Architect + AI VFX Director
4. 角色创作 → AI Hyperrealistic Character Artist
5. 叙事构建 → Multimodal Narrative Director
6. 音频制作 → Immersive Audio Architect + Master Composer + Master Voice Director
7. 合成输出 → AI-VFX Compositing Director
8. 实时制片 → Real-time VP Engineer
9. 资产归档 → Asset Library Curator
10. 合规审核 → AI Compliance Officer
```

### 快速响应模式

对于简单任务，根据任务类型直接调用对应大师：

- **图像生成**: Visual Universe Architect + AI Hyperrealistic Character Artist
- **视频制作**: AI VFX Director + AI-VFX Compositing Director
- **音频配乐**: Master Composer + Immersive Audio Architect
- **角色设计**: AI Hyperrealistic Character Artist + Chief Imagination Architect
- **工作流搭建**: AI Workflow TD + Chief AI Scientist
