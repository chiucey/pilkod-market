## 三、特殊情绪场景

### 3.1 孤独场景

#### 深夜街头独自行走

```
A solitary figure walking alone on empty rain-slicked city streets at 3am, street lamps casting long orange pools of light, the sound of distant traffic echoing in the silence.

The person's breath forms small clouds in the cold air. Traffic lights change for no one. A stray cat watches from under a parked car. The city feels abandoned, belonging only to the lonely walker and their thoughts.
```

#### 独自窗前凝望

```
A woman stands alone before a large window in a dimly lit apartment, watching the rain fall outside, her reflection ghost-like in the glass.

The apartment behind her is sparse and lonely - an unmade bed, dishes in the sink, a half-empty wine glass. But her attention is fixed on the storm outside, where lightning occasionally illuminates the dark cityscape. The window frames her like a portrait of solitude.
```

### 3.2 紧张对峙场景

#### 审讯室

```
A stark interrogation room with harsh fluorescent lighting, a metal table bolted to the floor, and a two-way mirror dominating one wall.

Two people sit across from each other - one in an authoritative position asking questions, the other maintaining composure despite the pressure. Sweat beads on one forehead despite the cold room. The clock on the wall ticks loudly. An evidence bag sits between them holding something damning.
```

#### 车祸现场

```
A chaotic highway accident scene at night, overturned vehicles with hazard lights blinking orange, emergency responders working frantically.

Broken glass covers the asphalt. A crumpled car door lies nearby. Paramedics work on a victim on a stretcher while police direct traffic around the debris. The look of shock on bystanders' faces tells the story of sudden tragedy interrupting ordinary lives.
```

### 3.3 梦幻场景

#### 梦境/想象

```
A surreal dreamscape where gravity doesn't apply - people float weightlessly through a corridor of giant white roses, petals swirling in slow motion around them.

Soft ethereal music seems to emanate from the light itself. The architecture bends impossibly, doorways leading to impossible spaces filled with golden light. The scene has a peaceful, surreal quality, like a half-remembered dream upon waking.
```

#### 回忆场景

```
A nostalgic memory scene rendered in slightly desaturated tones with soft focus edges, as if viewing the past through a veil of time.

Everything feels warmer and gentler than reality - the colors slightly yellowed like old photographs, voices heard as if underwater. A younger version of a character runs through a childhood backyard filled with summer sunlight. The memory slowly dissolves like morning fog as reality intrudes.
```

## 四、场景组合公式

### 4.1 快速组合模板

**公式**：基础场景 + 时间光线 + 情绪氛围 + 特殊元素

**示例**：

```
[基础场景：酒店豪华套房]
+ [黄昏/暖色光线]
+ [浪漫亲密氛围]
+ [玫瑰花瓣、香槟]

= 完整场景描述
```

### 4.2 完整场景描述生成示例

**需求**：霸总送女主回家的温馨场景

**组合要素**：
- 基础：女主公寓门口
- 时间：夜晚、雨后
- 光线：暖黄路灯
- 情绪：温馨、不舍
- 细节：湿漉漉的地面、朦胧雾气

**生成结果**：

```
A charming apartment building entrance on a quiet residential street after rain, warm yellow street lamp glow reflecting off wet cobblestones.

A tall handsome man in a coat stands near a beautiful woman at the building's door, neither seemingly ready to part. Mist rises gently from the warm ground. Her hand rests on the door handle while his hand hovers near her shoulder.

The wet pavement reflects the golden lamp light creating a romantic atmosphere. Above them, lit apartment windows suggest home and comfort waiting inside. The rain has washed everything clean, the air fresh and new.
```

## 五、场景描述质量检查

### 5.1 优秀场景描述标准

| 检查项 | 达标描述 | 未达标描述 |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| 具体性 | 「落地窗俯瞰黄浦江夜景」 | 「窗户很大，能看到外面」 |
| 感官性 | 「烤面包香气弥漫、阳光温暖」 | 「环境还可以」 |
| 情绪暗示 | 「空荡荡的餐桌只有一双筷子」 | 「他一个人吃饭」 |
| 视觉层次 | 「前景花瓣、中景人物、远景城市」 | 「他们站在一起」 |
| 光影氛围 | 「戏剧性侧光勾勒轮廓」 | 「灯光很亮」 |

### 5.2 常见问题修正

| 问题类型 | 错误示例 | 修正示例 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 太笼统 | 「一个漂亮的房间」 | 「白色极简公寓客厅，下午阳光透过纱帘洒落」 |
| 堆砌词藻 | 「金碧辉煌、富丽堂皇、美轮美奂」 | 「大理石地面反射着水晶吊灯的光芒」 |
| 忽略光影 | 「房间里有一些家具」 | 「昏暗的房间里，只有窗帘缝隙透进一束阳光」 |
| 情绪缺失 | 「他站在门口」 | 「他倚在门框上，双臂交叉，带着玩味的笑」 |
