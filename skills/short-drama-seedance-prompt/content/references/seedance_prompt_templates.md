## 六、提示词组合技巧

### 6.1 元素叠加原则

**基础结构**：
```
主体 + 场景 + 镜头运动 + 风格 + 氛围 + 光影 + 技术参数
```

**叠加示例**（从简到繁）：

1. **简单版**（快速生成）：
```
Beautiful woman in red qipao standing in traditional garden, cinematic lighting, romantic mood.
```

2. **进阶版**（标准质量）：
```
Elegant Chinese woman in flowing red qipao with golden embroidery, standing gracefully beside a koi pond in a classical Chinese garden at sunset.

Wide establishing shot slowly pushing in to medium close-up, capturing her serene expression and the koi swimming beneath lotus leaves.

Cinematic warm golden hour lighting with soft shadows, romantic atmosphere, traditional Chinese aesthetic.

4K, 24fps, 9:16 vertical, film-like quality.
```

3. **完整版**（最高质量）：
```
A breathtakingly beautiful woman in an elaborate traditional Chinese red qipao with intricate gold phoenix embroidery, her hair adorned with traditional hairpins, standing with poise beside an ancient koi pond in a classical Ming Dynasty garden.

The camera opens on a wide establishing shot capturing the full splendor of the pavilion, koi pond with cascading waterfall, willow trees swaying gently, then smoothly dollies forward through falling flower petals until reaching a medium close-up that frames her elegant profile against the water.

Volumetric golden hour sunlight streaming through the willow branches, creating dramatic god rays and lens flare, with cool blue reflections from the pond water providing contrast. Subtle incense smoke drifting through the frame adds to the ethereal quality.

Emotionally layered mood: serene yet expectant, beauty hiding ancient wisdom, the poetry of Chinese aesthetics coming to life, romance implied in every delicate detail.

4K Ultra HD cinematic quality, 24fps cinematic frame rate, 9:16 vertical format optimized for mobile viewing, anamorphic lens simulation for cinematic letterbox feel, film grain texture for organic quality, professional color grading with warm amber highlights and cool blue shadows.
```
