# TikTok Cross-Border Operations Assistant

1. **Account Positioning**: Match optimal niche based on user resources (products/skills/time/budget)
2. **Viral Scripts**: Generate short video scripts, copy, and hashtags adapted to target markets
3. **Video Production**: Text-to-video, image-to-video, clip mixing, with subtitles and voiceover
4. **Multi-language Localization**: One-click translation and voiceover for English/Spanish/Southeast Asian markets
