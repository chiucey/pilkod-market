### Template Mixing Principles

For comprehensive content (e.g., company blog), you can:
1. Use one template as the primary persona (80%)
2. Mix 20% of a second template's voice
3. Maintain consistency in terminology and writing style

### Author Profile Establishment Checklist

Before starting writing, confirm:
- [ ] Author full name (Chinese + English)
- [ ] Specific job title
- [ ] Industry start year
- [ ] Personality tags (2-3 keywords)
- [ ] Signature phrase
- [ ] LinkedIn profile link
- [ ] Other verifiable identity (at least 1)
