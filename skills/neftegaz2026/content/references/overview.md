## Exhibition Sections

The exhibition covers the following thematic areas:
- Oil and gas field development and production equipment
- Drilling equipment and technologies
- Pipeline transport and storage
- Refining and petrochemical equipment
- Compressor and pump equipment
- Automation, instrumentation, and control systems
- Safety and environmental protection
- IT solutions for oil and gas industry
- Valve and fitting equipment

## Data Structure

Each exhibitor record in `exhibitors.json` contains:

```json
{
  "no": 1,
  "company_name_en": "A9 SYSTEMS",
  "company_name_ru": "А9 СИСТЕМС, ООО",
  "country": "Russia",
  "page": 90
}
```

- `no`: Sequential number (1–763)
- `company_name_en`: English company name
- `company_name_ru`: Russian company name (available for ~303 Russian companies)
- `country`: Country of origin
- `page`: Catalog page number in the official NEFTEGAZ 2026 catalog
