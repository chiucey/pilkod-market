## 2. Scoring Interpretation

### Scoring Standards

| Total Score | Risk Level | Investment Type | Stock Allocation |
|-------------|------------|-----------------|------------------|
| 10-18 points | Conservative | Low risk | 10-20% |
| 19-26 points | Moderate | Low-medium risk | 20-35% |
| 27-34 points | Balanced | Medium risk | 35-50% |
| 35-42 points | Aggressive | Medium-high risk | 50-70% |
| 43-50 points | Highly aggressive | High risk | 70-90% |

### Investor Type Characteristics

#### Conservative Investor (10-18 points)

**Characteristics**:
- Low risk tolerance
- Investment goal is preservation first
- Cannot accept principal loss
- Prefers liquid products

**Recommended Allocation**:
- Government bonds, large-denomination CDs: 40-50%
- Money market funds, bank wealth management: 30-40%
- Pure bond funds: 10-20%
- Small amount of gold or bond funds: 5-10%

---

#### Moderate Investor (19-26 points)

**Characteristics**:
- Pursues stable returns
- Can accept small fluctuations
- Wants steady asset growth
- Has some awareness of risk

**Recommended Allocation**:
- Bond funds: 30-40%
- Money market funds, bank wealth management: 25-35%
- Hybrid funds: 15-25%
- Small amount of equity funds: 10-20%
- Gold/REITs: 5-10%

---

#### Balanced Investor (27-34 points)

**Characteristics**:
- Pursues balance between return and risk
- Can bear certain market fluctuations
- Has some investment experience
- Relatively long investment horizon

**Recommended Allocation**:
- Equity funds: 30-40%
- Bond funds: 25-30%
- Hybrid funds: 15-20%
- Money market funds: 10-15%
- Alternative investments: 5-10%

---

#### Aggressive Investor (35-42 points)

**Characteristics**:
- Pursues higher returns
- Can bear significant fluctuations
- Has extensive investment experience
- Long investment horizon

**Recommended Allocation**:
- Equity funds: 40-55%
- Growth stocks: 15-25%
- Bond funds: 15-20%
- Overseas assets: 10-15%
- Alternative investments: 5-10%

---

#### Highly Aggressive Investor (43-50 points)

**Characteristics**:
- Pursues maximum returns
- Can bear significant fluctuations including principal loss
- Has professional investment knowledge
- Has sufficient capital reserves

**Recommended Allocation**:
- Equity funds: 50-70%
- Growth/thematic investments: 20-30%
- Overseas stocks: 10-20%
- Alternative investments: 5-10%
- Small amount of cash management

---

## 3. Handling Special Situations

### Age and Risk Tolerance Relationship

| Age Range | Recommended Stock Ratio | Risk Control Points |
|-----------|------------------------|--------------------|
| 25-35 | 50-70% | Can withstand high volatility |
| 35-45 | 40-50% | Gradually increase defensive assets |
| 45-55 | 30-40% | Focus on asset safety |
| 55-65 | 20-30% | Primarily conservative |
| Over 65 | 10-20% | Conservative allocation |

### Occupation Impact on Risk Preference

**High-stability professions** (e.g., civil servants, teachers, doctors):
- Can appropriately increase stock ratio
- Risk tolerance score +5 points

**High-volatility professions** (e.g., sales, entrepreneurs):
- Suggest lowering stock ratio
- Risk tolerance score -5 points

### Asset Scale and Allocation Strategy

| Asset Scale | Strategy Focus | Allocation Characteristics |
|-------------|---------------|---------------------------|
| <1M | Simple and efficient | Primarily funds |
| 1M-3M | Diversified allocation | Moderate diversification |
| 3M-5M | Professional management | Consider private/separate accounts |
| >5M | Customized | Full-range asset allocation |

## 4. Adjustment Factors After Risk Assessment

### Bonus Items (Can upgrade risk level)

1. Rich investment experience (>5 years): +1 level
2. Stable and high income (>500K/year): +1 level
3. Long investment period (>10 years): +1 level
4. No household debt: +1 level
5. Has other assets/income sources: +1 level

### Deduction Items (Should downgrade risk level)

1. First-time investor: -1 level
2. Future major expenditure planned: -1 level
3. Heavy family burden (children's education, elderly care): -1 level
4. High liquidity needs: -1 level
5. Limited investment experience: -1 level

---

## 5. Regular Review Mechanism

### Review Frequency

- **Quarterly**: Check asset allocation ratios
- **Semi-annually**: Assess changes in risk tolerance
- **Annually**: Comprehensive portfolio review

### Triggers for Re-assessment

1. Major life events (marriage, childbirth, divorce, retirement)
2. Significant income changes
3. Serious illness or accident
4. Extreme market conditions
5. Changes in investment goals

### Rebalancing Thresholds

| Asset Category | Deviation Threshold | Adjustment Method |
|---------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Stocks/Funds | +/-5% | Staggered adjustment |
| Bond funds | +/-5% | One-time adjustment |
| Money market funds | +/-10% | Automatic adjustment |
| Alternative investments | +/-3% | Cautious adjustment |

## 6. Risk Disclosures

### Important Disclaimers

1. **Limitations of Risk Assessment**:
   - Questionnaire is only for reference, cannot fully reflect true risk tolerance
   - Market environment and personal circumstances may change
   - Suggest combining professional financial advisor opinions

2. **Market Risk**:
   - All investments have risk of principal loss
   - Past performance does not indicate future results
   - Market volatility is normal

3. **Liquidity Risk**:
   - Some products may have lock-up periods
   - May not be able to liquidate promptly in extreme situations
   - Confirm capital use plan before investing

4. **Inflation Risk**:
   - Excessive conservatism may lead to decreased purchasing power
   - Need to find balance between risk and return
