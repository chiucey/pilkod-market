# Investment Products Reference Guide

## 1. Equity Investment Products

### 1. Index Funds (ETF)

| Product Name | Code | Features | Suitable For |
|--------------|------|----------|--------------|
| CSI 300 ETF | 510300 | Represents A-share blue chips | Moderate investors |
| CSI 500 ETF | 510500 | Represents mid-cap growth | Balanced investors |
| ChiNext ETF | 159915 | Tracks ChiNext index | Aggressive investors |
| STAR 50 ETF | 588000 | Science and technology innovation | High risk tolerance |
| Nasdaq ETF | 513100 | US technology stocks | Overseas allocation needs |

### 2. Actively Managed Funds

**Stock Fund Selection Criteria**:
- Operating history > 5 years
- AUM > 1 billion
- Stable fund manager
- Long-term performance in top 1/3
- Sharpe ratio > 0.5

**Recommended Fund Types**:
- Value funds: Suitable for conservative investors
- Growth funds: Suitable for young, high risk tolerance
- Balanced funds: Suitable for most investors

### 3. Direct Stock Investment

**Blue Chip Selection Criteria**:
- 5 consecutive years of profitability
- Stable dividends
- Industry leader
- Reasonable valuation (PE < industry average)

**Sector Allocation Recommendations**:
- Consumer (F&B, appliances): 30%
- Finance (banks, insurance): 20%
- Technology (internet, electronics): 25%
- Healthcare: 15%
- Others: 10%

## 2. Fixed Income Products

### 1. Bond Funds

| Type | Risk Level | Expected Return | Suitable For |
|------|------------|-----------------|--------------|
| Pure bond fund | Low | 3-5% | Conservative |
| Secondary bond fund | Low-medium | 5-8% | Moderate |
| Convertible bond fund | Medium | 8-12% | Balanced |

### 2. Government Bonds

- Savings bonds: 3-year and 5-year terms, interest rate slightly higher than same-term deposits
- Book-entry bonds: Tradeable, good liquidity
- Electronic bonds: Annual interest payment

### 3. Bank Wealth Management

- R1 (Low risk): Primarily money market investments
- R2 (Low-medium risk): Primarily fixed income
- R3 (Medium risk): May invest small amounts of stocks

## 3. Cash Management Products

| Product | Features | Return | Liquidity |
|---------|----------|--------|-----------|
| Money market fund | Extremely low risk | ~2% | T+1 redemption |
| Treasury reverse repo | Short-term wealth management | 2-5% | Efficient capital use |
| Bank T+0 | Real-time redemption | 2-3% | Immediate arrival |
| Innovative deposits | Principal guaranteed | 3-4% | Flexible terms |

## 4. Alternative Investment Products

### 1. Gold Investment

| Method | Features | Suitable Scenario |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| Physical gold | Store of value, inconvenient | Long-term allocation |
| Gold ETF | Convenient trading | Short-term operations |
| Paper gold | Bank account trading | Investment hedge |

### 2. REITs (Real Estate Investment Trust)

**Public REITs Advantages**:
- Low threshold (starting from 100 RMB)
- Professional management
- Good liquidity
- Stable dividends (>90% of distributable income)

**Recommended Allocation**: 5-10% of total investment

## 5. Insurance Products

### 1. Protection Insurance

| Type | Main Function | Recommended Coverage |
|------|---------------|---------------------|
| Term life | Death protection | 10x annual income |
| Critical illness | Major disease protection | 300K-500K |
| Medical insurance | Medical reimbursement | 1M+ |
| Accident insurance | Accident injury | 1M+ |

### 2. Savings Insurance

- Annuity insurance: Retirement planning
- Participating insurance: Asset inheritance
- Universal life: Flexible deposit and withdrawal

### 3. Insurance Allocation Principles

1. **Protection before savings**: Prioritize protection insurance first
2. **Adults before children**: Primary breadwinners first
3. **Premium expenditure**: Should not exceed 10% of annual household income
4. **Coverage determination**: Cover household debt and 5-year expenses

## 6. Overseas Allocation Products

| HK stock QDII | Invest in HK stock market | Medium |
| Global bond QDII | Invest in global bonds | Medium-low |

### 2. Cross-border Wealth Management Connect

- Southbound: Mainland invest in HK/Macau products
- Northbound: Overseas invest in mainland products
- Quota limit: 3M per person per year

## 7. Product Selection Checklist

### Equity Products Screening

```
[ ] Fund manager has complete qualifications
[ ] Product operating history > 3 years
[ ] Fund manager has rich experience
[ ] Historical performance stable
[ ] Fee reasonable (management fee < 1.5%)
[ ] Appropriate scale (200M-10B)
[ ] Good risk control
[ ] Timely information disclosure
```

### Fixed Income Products Screening

```
[ ] Issuer has good credit
[ ] Historical redemption records normal
[ ] Guarantee measures complete
[ ] Liquidity arrangement reasonable
[ ] Return meets market levels
[ ] Term matches capital needs
```

## 8. Product Risk Level Reference

| Risk Level | Product Type | Max Loss Potential | Suitable For |
|------------|-------------|-------------------|--------------|
| R1 | Government bonds, deposits | 0% | Extremely conservative |

| R3 | Secondary bond funds, balanced funds | <20% | Moderate |
| R4 | Stock funds, stocks | <50% | Balanced |
| R5 | Futures, leveraged products | >50% | Aggressive |
