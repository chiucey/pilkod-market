# Robust Investment Advisory Skill

Generate scientific and robust asset allocation advice based on user's investment amount and risk preference.

## Investment Amount Tiers

### Tier Definitions

| Tier | Amount Range | Investment Characteristics |
|------|--------------|---------------------------|
| Entry-level | 500K - 1M | Seek stability, focus on low-risk products |
| Intermediate | 1M - 3M | Moderate diversification, can tolerate volatility |
| Advanced | 3M - 5M | Professional allocation, pursue long-term growth |
| High Net Worth | 5M+ | Customized multi-strategy allocation |

## Risk Assessment Dimensions

### Risk Tolerance Assessment

#### 1. Age Factor

| Age Range | Stock Allocation | Notes |
|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| Under 30 | 60-70% | Can withstand high volatility |
| 30-45 | 40-50% | Moderate risk |
| 45-55 | 30-40% | Focus on stability |
| Over 55 | Below 20% | Conservative allocation |

#### 2. Income Stability

- **Stable income**: Can increase stock allocation
- **Variable income**: Increase bond and cash allocation

#### 3. Investment Experience

- **Beginner**: Start with funds, learn gradually
- **Experienced**: Flexible allocation based on judgment

#### 4. Liquidity Needs

- **Large expenditure soon**: Increase money market fund allocation
- **Long-term untouched**: Can increase equity allocation

```
## Risk Disclosures
1. Markets involve risk; invest with caution
2. Diversification can reduce non-systematic risk
3. Regular review and portfolio rebalancing recommended
4. Do not invest all funds in a single asset

## Implementation Steps

### Phase 1 (Week 1)
- Open securities account
- Complete risk assessment
- Transfer funds

### Phase 2 (Week 2)
- Allocate bonds and money funds (40%)
- Arrange insurance coverage

### Phase 3 (Week 3-4)
- Stagger build equity positions (using dollar-cost averaging)
- Establish alternative investment positions

### Ongoing Maintenance
- Monthly: Review performance
- Quarterly: Evaluate allocation ratios
- Annually: Complete portfolio rebalancing

## Rebalancing Strategy

### Rebalancing Triggers
- Single asset deviates from target allocation by more than 5%
- Annual scheduled review
- Major market changes

### Rebalancing Methods
- Sell assets exceeding targets
- Buy assets below targets
- Or use dollar-cost averaging for gradual adjustment

---

**Disclaimer**: This recommendation is for reference only and does not constitute investment advice. All investments involve risk. Please consult a professional financial advisor before making investment decisions.
```

## Usage Process

1. **Collect Information**: Confirm investment amount, risk preference, investment horizon
2. **Analysis & Assessment**: Adjust allocation based on age, income, experience factors
3. **Generate Plan**: Output standard-formatted investment recommendations
4. **Risk Disclosure**: Clearly state investment risks and precautions
5. **Implementation Advice**: Provide phased execution steps

## Quality Control

- Ensure allocation percentages sum to 100%
- Adjust recommendations based on individual circumstances
- Provide specific, actionable product recommendations
- Emphasize importance of diversification
- Include complete risk disclosures
