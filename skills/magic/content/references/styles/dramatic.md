# Dramatic Style

High-contrast, emotionally intense comic style for powerful narratives.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Line work**: Bold, dynamic lines, heavy shadows
- **Color palette**: High contrast, dramatic lighting
- **Shading**: Heavy chiaroscuro, deep blacks, bright highlights
- **Composition**: Dynamic angles, extreme close-ups, kinetic layouts
- **Character design**: Exaggerated expressions for emotional impact

## Best For

- Conflict and breakthrough stories
- Emotional arcs and tragedies
- Revolutionary moments
- High-stakes narratives

## Example Prompts

```
Dramatic comic style, heavy chiaroscuro lighting, bold shadows.
High contrast image with deep blacks and bright highlights.
Extreme close-up on [character]'s intense expression.
Dutch angle composition, emotional intensity.
```
