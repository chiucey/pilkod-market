# Shoujo Style

Japanese romance manga style with delicate features and emotional expression.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Line work**: Fine, delicate lines with detailed hair and eyes
- **Color palette**: Soft pastels, sparkles, flower petal motifs
- **Shading**: Soft gradients, emphasis on expressive eyes
- **Composition**: Close-ups on emotional moments, decorative panels
- **Character design**: Large expressive eyes, delicate features, elegant poses

## Best For

- Romance and love stories
- Emotional narratives
- Friendship and relationships
- Youth-oriented sentimental content

## Example Prompts

```
Japanese shoujo manga style, delicate fine lines.
Large expressive eyes with detailed highlights.
Soft pastel color palette with sparkles.
Flower petal motifs and decorative panel borders.
Emotional close-ups with gentle, elegant character design.
```
