# Vibrant Style

Bold, energetic style with saturated colors and dynamic energy.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Line work**: Bold outlines, confident thick lines
- **Color palette**: Highly saturated, bright and punchy colors
- **Shading**: High contrast with vibrant highlights
- **Composition**: Dynamic layouts, energetic compositions
- **Character design**: Exaggerated features, high-energy poses

## Best For

- Modern content
- High-energy narratives
- Action and excitement
- Youth-oriented topics

## Example Prompts

```
Vibrant comic style with highly saturated colors.
Bold outlines and high energy composition.
Bright, punchy color palette.
Dynamic poses and energetic character expressions.
Pop art influence, maximum visual impact.
```
