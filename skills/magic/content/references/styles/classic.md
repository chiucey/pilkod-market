# Classic Style

Balanced, timeless comic style suitable for most content types.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Line work**: Clean, confident lines, moderate detail
- **Color palette**: Balanced saturation, naturalistic colors
- **Shading**: Standard comic book shading (cell shading)
- **Composition**: Traditional panel layouts, balanced framing
- **Character design**: Clear silhouettes, expressive but realistic proportions

## Best For

- Biographies and historical content
- General storytelling
- Mixed-emotion narratives
- Professional but approachable topics

## Example Prompts

```
Classic comic style, clean ink lines, balanced colors.
Professional portrait of [character], realistic proportions.
Standard panel layout with clear visual hierarchy.
Natural lighting, moderate contrast.
```
