# Realistic Style

Photorealistic or highly detailed art approach for professional, mature content.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Line work**: Fine, detailed lines or painterly approach
- **Color palette**: Naturalistic, accurate to real-world lighting
- **Shading**: Realistic rendering with depth and texture
- **Composition**: Cinematic framing, film-like composition
- **Character design**: Realistic proportions, detailed textures

## Best For

- Professional and business content
- Wine, food, lifestyle topics
- Mature narratives
- Documentary-style stories

## Example Prompts

```
Realistic comic art style, painterly rendering.
Naturalistic lighting and accurate colors.
Cinematic composition with film-like framing.
Detailed textures on [character] clothing and environment.
Professional, mature aesthetic.
```
