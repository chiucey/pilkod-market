# Sepia Style

Nostalgic, vintage aesthetic evoking aged photographs and historical documents.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Line work**: Monochromatic or limited sepia palette
- **Color palette**: Browns, tans, warm grays (aged photograph look)
- **Shading**: Tonal range within sepia spectrum
- **Composition**: Cinematic framing, portrait orientation
- **Character design**: Period-appropriate clothing, timeless quality

## Best For

- Pre-1950s content
- Classical and historical stories
- Biographies of historical figures
- Nostalgic, memoir-style narratives

```
Sepia photograph style, monochromatic brown tones.
```
