# Warm Style

Soft, approachable style with emotional resonance and gentle aesthetics.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Line work**: Softer edges, gentle linework
- **Color palette**: Warm tones (yellows, oranges, soft reds), cozy atmosphere
- **Shading**: Soft gradients, gentle transitions
- **Composition**: Intimate framing, close character interactions
- **Character design**: Friendly expressions, rounded features

## Best For

- Personal stories and memoirs
- Mentorship narratives
- Emotional journeys
- Relationship-focused content

## Example Prompts

```
Warm comic style, soft colors with yellow and orange tones.
Gentle lighting creating cozy atmosphere.
Soft edges and rounded shapes on [character].
Intimate composition with friendly expressions.
```
