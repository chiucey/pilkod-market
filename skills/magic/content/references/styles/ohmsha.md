# Ohmsha Style

Japanese educational manga style optimized for learning and explanation.

See `ohmsha-guide.md` for comprehensive guidelines.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Line work**: Clean manga-style linework, clear readability
- **Color palette**: Bright, approachable colors with clear visual hierarchy
- **Shading**: Minimal to moderate shading, focus on clarity
- **Composition**: Varied panel sizes for emphasis, often webtoon format
- **Character design**: Doraemon cast (by default) or custom, expressive and clear

## Special Requirements

- **Visual metaphors**: Every concept must be visualized as gadget/action
- **No talking heads**: Characters must act, not just explain
- **Character roles**: Doraemon (mentor), Nobita (learner), Gian (challenge), Shizuka (clarifier)
- **Gadget system**: Concepts become physical objects that transform

## Best For

- Tutorials and how-to content
- Technical explanations (programming, AI, science)
- Educational content
- Concept introductions
