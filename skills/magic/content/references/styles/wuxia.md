# Wuxia Style

Chinese martial arts fantasy style with flowing movement and atmospheric effects.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Line work**: Flowing, dynamic lines with calligraphic influence
- **Color palette**: Rich colors, atmospheric effects (mist, energy)
- **Shading**: Dramatic with special effects (qi flow, weapon trails)
- **Composition**: Sweeping landscapes, dynamic action poses
- **Character design**: Period clothing (hanfu, robes), flowing hair and fabric

## Best For

- Martial arts stories
- Wuxia and xianxia content
- Chinese historical fantasy
- Action-packed narratives
