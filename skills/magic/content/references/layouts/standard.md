# Standard Layout

Traditional comic grid layout for balanced storytelling.

## Characteristics

- 4-6 panels per page
- Grid-based (2x2, 2x3, or 3x2)
- Equal or balanced panel sizes
- Left-to-right, top-to-bottom flow
- Predictable and comfortable reading

## Panel Distribution

- 2x2 grid: 4 panels, good for dialogue
- 2x3 grid: 6 panels, balanced narrative
- 3x2 grid: 6 panels, wider aspect focus
- Mixed: Some pages 4 panels, some 6

## Best For

- General storytelling
- Dialogue-heavy scenes
- Balanced narratives
- Traditional comic formats

## Example

```
┌─────┬─────┐
│  1  │  2  │
├─────┼─────┤
│  3  │  4  │
└─────┴─────┘

Or:

┌─────┬─────┬─────┐
│  1  │  2  │  3  │
├─────┼─────┼─────┤
│  4  │  5  │  6  │
└─────┴─────┴─────┘
```
