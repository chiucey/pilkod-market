# Mixed Layout

Flexible approach combining different layouts throughout the comic.

## Characteristics

- Variable panel counts (2-8 panels per page)
- Page-by-page layout decisions
- Splash pages for key moments
- Dense pages for explanations
- Variety maintains interest

## Layout Strategy

- Opening: Cinematic/splash for impact
- Exposition: Standard or dense for information
- Action: Splash or cinematic for drama
- Dialogue: Standard for character interaction
- Climax: Splash for maximum impact
- Resolution: Cinematic for closure

## Best For

- Long-form stories (15+ pages)
- Complex narratives
- Variations in pacing
- Most biographies and documentaries

## Example Strategy

```
Page 1: Splash (cover/hook)
Pages 2-3: Cinematic (establishing)
Pages 4-8: Mixed (build story)
Page 9: Splash (turning point)
Pages 10-13: Mixed (climax builds)
Page 14: Full splash (climax)
Page 15: Cinematic (resolution)
```
