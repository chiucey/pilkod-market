# Tag Taxonomy

Use exactly these level-1 and level-2 tags unless the user explicitly asks to extend the framework.

## 1. 疾病背景

- 疾病定义与发病机制
- 流行病学数据
- 疾病负担分析
- 诊断与治疗现状
- 指南推荐

Typical card focus: disease concept, immunopathogenesis, epidemiology, burden, clinical status, guideline baseline.

## 2. 市场需求

- 未满足的临床需求
- 患者画像
- 患者人群特征
- 诊疗路径
- 治疗选择
- 患者流
- 疾病与管理
- 目标科室
- 医生观念
- 处方习惯

Typical card focus: unmet need, patient journey, target department, treatment pathway, decision factors, adherence, access and management gaps.

## 3. 推广策略

- 产品定位
- 核心信息点
- 医学故事

Typical card focus: product positioning, key message, medical narrative, education angle. These cards almost always need compliance review.

## 4. 产品证据链

- 药品成分与作用机理
- 各期临床试验数据
- 有效性分析
- 安全性分析
- 特殊人群与合并用药
- 依从性分析
- 经济学分析

Typical card focus: MOA, clinical development, efficacy, safety, subgroup, special population, adherence, pharmacoeconomics.

## 5. 竞品分析

- SWOT分析（优势、劣势、机会、威胁）
- 差异化医学故事
- 竞品概况（产品、种类、上市时间、作用机制）
- 竞争产品对比（疗效、安全性、依从性、经济学对比）

Typical card focus: competitor landscape, comparative evidence, differentiation, SWOT. These cards always need compliance review.

## 6. 法律法规

- 法规变化（医保、普惠采购政策等）
- 药品使用与管理

Typical card focus: policy change, reimbursement, procurement, labeling, drug-use restrictions, management requirements.

## Evidence Level Values

- High: well-conducted RCT, high-quality systematic review/meta-analysis of RCTs, or strong guideline recommendation supported by high-quality evidence.
- Moderate: prospective cohort, strong real-world study, externally validated diagnostic/prognostic model, or consistent observational evidence.
- Low: retrospective study, case-control, cross-sectional, exploratory subgroup, small trial, internal-only model validation.
- Preclinical: animal/cell/organoid/mechanistic evidence.
- Expert/Review: narrative review, expert opinion, consensus without clearly graded evidence.
- Regulatory/Policy: official regulatory, policy, reimbursement, or management document.
