# 宠物配饰图片生成提示词模板

## 白底产品图模板

```
Photorealistic product photo of [产品名称].
[材质描述]. [形状/造型描述]. [颜色描述].
[细节特征]. Clean white background, studio lighting, high definition, 1:1 aspect ratio.
```

**示例**：
```
Photorealistic product photo of an elegant white swan-shaped headband for pets.
Made of soft white faux fur material with delicate feather textures.
Small elegant swan design perched on headband.
Elastic band at the base for secure pet head attachment.
Clean white background, studio lighting, high definition, 1:1 aspect ratio.
```

## 批量生成提示词结构

当从表格/文件中提取描述时，按以下顺序构建提示词：

1. **开头**：`Photorealistic photo of`
2. **主体**：`[宠物品种] wearing [配饰名称]`
3. **材质**：`[材质特征]`
4. **造型**：`[形状/设计特征]`
5. **细节**：`[装饰/功能细节]`
6. **氛围**：`[宠物表情/动作]`
7. **背景**：`[背景描述]`
8. **光照**：`natural/studio lighting`
9. **规格**：`high definition, 1:1 aspect ratio`
