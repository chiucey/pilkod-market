## Presentation Opening Techniques

| Technique | Best For | Example |
|------------|----------|---------|
| Question | Engagement | "Have you ever wondered...?" |
| Statistic | Impact | "90% of companies fail at..." |
| Story | Connection | "Let me tell you about..." |
| Quote | Credibility | "As Steve Jobs said..." |
| Visual | Surprise | Start with striking image |
| Problem | Urgency | "The biggest challenge is..." |
