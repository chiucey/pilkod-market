# Vendor Risk Assessment - Data Sources Reference

## Official Government Platforms

### 1. National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System
- **URL**: https://www.gsxt.gov.cn
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Company basic registration information
  - Annual reports (2013-present)
  - Administrative licenses
  - Administrative penalties
  - Change records
  - Equity pledge records
  - Administrative licensing records
- **Access**: Public, no registration required

### 2. China Judgements Online
- **URL**: https://www.court.gov.cn
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Dishonest persons subject to enforcement (Shixin Beizhixing Ren)
  - Persons subject to consumption restrictions (Xianzhi Gaoxiaofei)
  - Enforcement case information
  - Auction information
- **Access**: Public, no registration required

### 3. Credit China
- **URL**: https://www.creditchina.gov.cn
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Credit violations
  - Red and black list records
  - Tax violations
  - Food safety violations
  - Environmental violations
  - Import/export violations
- **Access**: Public, no registration required

### 4. State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR)
- **URL**: https://www.samr.gov.cn
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Product quality supervision
  - Administrative penalties
  - Business registration changes
- **Access**: Public information

### 5. Ministry of Finance Government Procurement
- **URL**: https://www.ccgp.gov.cn
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Government procurement blacklists
  - Procurement violation records
  - Bidding violations
- **Access**: Public

### 6. State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission
- **URL**: http://www.sasac.gov.cn
- **Data Coverage**:
  - State-owned enterprise supervision
  - Procurement regulations
  - Compliance guidelines
- **Access**: Public

### 7. China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC)
- **URL**: https://www.csrc.gov.cn
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Listed company disciplinary actions
  - Securities violations
  - Regulatory penalties
- **Access**: Public

### 8. Ministry of Commerce
- **URL**: https://www.mofcom.gov.cn
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Import/export violations
  - Commercial credit records
  - Foreign trade compliance
- **Access**: Public

### 9. State Administration of Taxation
- **URL**: https://www.chinatax.gov.cn
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Tax violation records
  - Tax credit rating
  - Invoice irregularities
- **Access**: Some public, some requires login

### 10. Military Procurement Platforms
- **URL**: Various military procurement portals
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Military procurement blacklist (Jungui Caigou Weigui)
  - Equipment procurement restrictions
  - Defense industry compliance
- **Access**: Public announcements

## Third-Party Credit Platforms

### 1. Tianyancha
- **URL**: https://www.tianyancha.com
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Company comprehensive profiles
  - Shareholder information
  - Legal disputes
  - Risk warnings
  - Business changes
  - Financial information
- **API Available**: Yes (requires subscription)
- **Free Tier**: Limited queries

### 2. Qichacha (QCQC)
- **URL**: https://www.qcc.com
- **Data Coverage**:
  - Company registration details
  - Shareholding structure
  - Legal cases
  - Risk indicators
  - Financial data
- **API Available**: Yes (requires subscription)
- **Free Tier**: Limited queries

## Query Strategy

### Recommended Priority Order

1. **Step 1**: National Enterprise Credit System - Basic verification
2. **Step 2**: China Judgements Online - Enforcement check
3. **Step 3**: Credit China - Overall credit status
4. **Step 4**: Tianyancha/QCQC - Comprehensive risk overview
5. **Step 5**: Government procurement platforms - Procurement compliance
6. **Step 6**: Military procurement (if applicable) - Defense restrictions
7. **Step 7**: Listed company platforms (if applicable) - Regulatory compliance

### Multi-Source Cross-Reference

Always cross-reference findings across multiple sources:
- Verify enforcement records through multiple government platforms
- Compare third-party platform data with official sources
- Note any discrepancies for further investigation
