# 漫画提示词参考手册

## 通用风格关键词

### 日系漫画风格
- `manga style, black and white, screentone`
- `anime style, vibrant colors, cel shading`
- `shonen manga, action lines, speed lines`
- `shoujo manga, floral background, soft colors`

### 欧美漫画风格
- `comic book style, bold outlines, halftone dots`
- `american comic, flat colors, strong shadows`
- `european comic style, ligne claire, clean lines`

### 中国风漫画
- `chinese ink painting style, traditional manga`
- `chinese comic style, modern manhua`
- `wuxia manga style, traditional chinese painting`

### 特殊风格
- `cyberpunk manga style, neon lights, dark background`
- `watercolor illustration style, soft edges`
- `noir comic style, high contrast, shadows`
- `chibi style, cute, small characters`
- `sketch style, rough lines, artistic`

---

## 构图关键词

- `close-up shot` — 特写
- `medium shot` — 中景
- `full body shot` — 全身
- `wide shot` — 远景
- `over the shoulder shot` — 过肩镜头
- `bird's eye view` — 俯视
- `low angle` — 仰视
- `dutch angle` — 倾斜镜头

---

## 情感/氛围关键词

- `happy atmosphere, bright` — 欢乐、明亮
- `melancholic, rainy day` — 忧郁、雨天
- `tense atmosphere, dramatic lighting` — 紧张、戏剧性光照
- `peaceful, soft sunlight` — 宁静、柔光
- `action scene, motion blur` — 动作场面、运动模糊
- `romantic, sunset` — 浪漫、日落

## 漫画专用术语

- `comic panel` — 漫画面板
- `speech bubble` — 对话气泡
- `thought bubble` — 想法气泡
- `sound effects, onomatopoeia` — 音效词
- `speed lines` — 速度线
- `action lines` — 动作线
- `screentone shading` — 网点阴影
- `hatching` — 排线阴影
- `white space` — 留白

## 提示词模板

### 模板1：角色对话场景
```
{manga style}, {character description}, {action/emotion},
talking to another character, {background}, speech bubble visible,
{lighting/mood}, comic panel
```

### 模板2：动作场景
```
{manga style}, {character description}, {action pose},
motion blur, speed lines, dynamic angle, {background},
intense atmosphere, comic panel
```

### 模板3：情感场景
```
{manga style}, {character description},
{emotion: crying/smiling/etc.}, close-up shot,
{background/atmosphere}, soft lighting,
screentone shading, comic panel
```

---

## 负面提示词（避免的问题）

- `photorealistic` — 避免照片感
- `3D render` — 避免3D渲染风格
- `ugly, deformed` — 避免变形
- `blurry, low quality` — 避免模糊
- `extra limbs` — 避免多余肢体
- `bad anatomy` — 避免解剖错误
