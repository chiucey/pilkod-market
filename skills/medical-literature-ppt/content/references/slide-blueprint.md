# Default slide blueprint

Use this as a planning scaffold. Adapt to the source article while staying within 20 slides.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Title slide**
   - Chinese title derived from the paper title
   - subtitle with journal / year / study type if useful
   - citation footer at lower left

2. **Clinical background / unmet need**
   - why the study matters
   - one source-derived background figure/table only if present in the paper

3. **Study objective and key question**
   - PICO-style summary if applicable

4. **Study design**
   - design schematic, timeline, arms, endpoints

5. **Population / baseline / methods**
   - inclusion-exclusion, sample size, baseline table, assay or evaluation method

6-16. **Results**
   - allocate most slides here
   - cover every original figure and table
   - group by endpoint family: efficacy, safety, subgroup, biomarker, mechanistic, exploratory
   - for each result slide, make the title the clinical takeaway

17-18. **Interpretation / strengths / limitations**
   - faithful to discussion section
   - do not overclaim

19. **Key summary**
   - 3 concise Chinese takeaways

20. **Optional closing / appendix-like dense table slide**
   - use only if needed to keep complete figure/table coverage

## Slide composition rules

- Prefer one dominant visual plus one short text block.
- When combining two visuals, make the comparison explicit in the title and subtitle.
- Use callout text sparingly; it must come from or be directly supported by the paper.
- Dense numeric slides should reduce text rather than shrinking charts too aggressively.

## Figure/table coverage checklist

Before finalizing, privately confirm:
- Figure 1 covered
- Figure 2 covered
- ...
- Table 1 covered
- Table 2 covered
- all subpanels and clinically important footnotes preserved where necessary
