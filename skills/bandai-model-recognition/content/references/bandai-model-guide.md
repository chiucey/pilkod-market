# Bandai Model Product Line Complete Reference

## I. Gundam Plamodel Series (Gunpla)

### 1. HG Series (High Grade)
- **Scale**: 1/144
- **Difficulty**: ⭐ Entry
- **Features**:
  - Simple assembly, suitable for beginners
  - Affordable price (¥400-800)
  - Some products have inner frame details
  - Many stickers included

### 2. HGUC Series (High Grade Universal Century)
- **Scale**: 1/144
- **Features**:
  - UC era authentic works
  - Rigorous research and accuracy
  - Classic mobile suits primarily

### 3. HGCE Series (High Grade Cosmic Era)
- **Scale**: 1/144
- **Features**:
  - SEED era works
  - Excellent joint design

### 4. HGBD Series (High Grade Build Divers)
- **Scale**: 1/144
- **Features**:
  - Build series exclusive scale
  - High compatibility

### 5. MG Series (Master Grade)
- **Scale**: 1/100
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ Intermediate
- **Features**:
  - Fine inner frame skeleton
  - Excellent joint articulation
  - Moderate price (¥1,500-3,000)
  - Top choice for collectors

### 6. RG Series (Real Grade)
- **Scale**: 1/144
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Advanced
- **Features**:
  - Precise inner frame skeleton
  - High detail at small scale
  - Reduced stickers, dry transfers preferred
  - High collection value
  - Price (¥1,800-3,500)

### 7. PG Series (Perfect Grade)
- **Scale**: 1/60
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Master
- **Features**:
  - Complete inner frame skeleton
  - Motorized drive (some models)
  - Ultra-high detail
  - Expensive (¥8,000-25,000)
  - Collector's treasure

### 8. MEGA Series (MEGA SIZE)
- **Scale**: 1/48
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐ Intermediate
- **Features**:
  - Large scale display type
  - Relatively simple assembly
  - Price (¥4,000-6,000)

## III. Common Product Number Rules

### Gundam Model Numbering
```
RG-##   → Real Grade numbering
MG-###  → Master Grade numbering (e.g., MG-001, MG-100)
HG-###  → High Grade numbering
PG-##   → Perfect Grade numbering
```

### Limited Edition Identifiers
```
PB      → Premium Bandai Limited
Gundam- → Gunpla supplies
CE      → Limited color version
```

## VI. FAQ

### Q: HG and RG are both 1/144, which to choose?
A:
- RG has finer details and inner frame skeleton
- HG has more affordable price for bulk collecting
- RG assembly difficulty is slightly higher

### Q: What's the difference between MG and PG?
A:
- PG has larger scale (1/60 vs 1/100)
- PG has more complete inner frame, some with motorization
- Price difference is about 3-5x

### Q: What is PB Limited?
A: Premium Bandai online exclusive
- Only available at official website
- Usually more refined or special colors
- Slightly higher price, requires lottery/reservation
