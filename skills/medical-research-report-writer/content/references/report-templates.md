# Medical Research Report Template Library

## Clinical Study Report Templates

### RCT Study Report (CONSORT Guidelines)

```markdown
# [Study Title]: A [Design] Randomized Controlled Trial of [Intervention] versus [Control] in [Condition] Patients

## Abstract

**Background**: [Brief background and objectives]

**Objective**: To evaluate the effect of [intervention] compared with [control] on [primary outcome] in [condition] patients

**Methods**:
- Study Design: Randomized Controlled Trial
- Study Centers: [Number] centers
- Patients: [Diagnostic criteria] patients aged [range], n=[N]
- Randomization Ratio: 1:1
- Intervention Group: [Specific intervention including dose, route, duration]
- Control Group: [Control measure]
- Primary Outcome: [Measure and timepoints]
- Secondary Outcomes: [List]

**Results**:
- Patients Analyzed: [N]
- Baseline: Groups comparable at baseline
- Primary Outcome: [Effect size, 95%CI, P-value]
- Secondary Outcomes: [Brief results]
- Adverse Events: [Incidence and main types]

**Conclusion**: [Main conclusions and clinical significance]

**Keywords**: [5-8 keywords]

## Main Text

### 1. Introduction

#### 1.1 Background
[Detailed disease background, existing treatments, and limitations]

#### 1.2 Objectives
[Clear research objectives]

#### 1.3 Hypotheses
[List hypotheses if applicable]

### 2. Methods

#### 2.1 Study Design
[Describe study design including randomization method, blinding]

#### 2.2 Participants
- Inclusion Criteria:
  1. [Criterion 1]
  2. [Criterion 2]
- Exclusion Criteria:
  1. [Criterion 1]
  2. [Criterion 2]
- Withdrawal Criteria:

#### 2.3 Interventions
| Parameter | Intervention Group | Control Group |
|----------|-------------------|--------------|
| Drug/Measure | [Name] | [Name] |
| Dose | [Dose] | [Dose] |
| Route | [Route] | [Route] |
| Duration | [Duration] | [Duration] |

#### 2.4 Outcome Measures
- **Primary Outcome**: [Definition and measurement]
- **Secondary Outcomes**: [List]
- **Safety Measures**: Adverse events, vital signs, lab tests

#### 2.5 Sample Size Calculation
[Describe sample size calculation basis]

#### 2.6 Statistical Analysis
- Analysis Sets: [FAS/PPS/SS]
- Primary Analysis Method: [Method]
- Missing Data Handling: [Method]

### 3. Results

#### 3.1 Patient Flow [CONSORT flowchart]

#### 3.2 Baseline Characteristics
| Characteristic | Intervention (n=X) | Control (n=X) |
|---------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Age (years) | X±X | X±X |
| Sex (M/F) | X/X | X/X |
| [Other] | ... | ... |

#### 3.3 Primary Outcome
[Detailed results including effect size, 95%CI, P-value]

#### 3.4 Secondary Outcomes
[Detailed results]

#### 3.5 Safety Results
[Adverse event summary]

### 4. Discussion

#### 4.1 Key Findings
[Summarize main results]

#### 4.2 Comparison with Literature
[Compare with relevant studies]

#### 4.3 Clinical Implications
[Explain clinical application value]

#### 4.4 Limitations
[Honestly discuss limitations]

#### 4.5 Conclusions
[Concise conclusions]

### 5. References
[By format]

### Appendices
- Supplementary Tables
- Supplementary Figures
- Protocol Deviations
```
