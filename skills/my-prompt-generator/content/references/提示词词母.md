* (key light: soft / hard / diffused)
* (fill light: subtle / minimal / absent)
* (rim light / backlight)

* (ambient light presence)
* (practical light visible in scene: lamp, window, candle)

👉 决定整体可读性 vs 戏剧性

### 2️⃣ 光源方向（Light Direction）

👉 决定五官"立不立体"

👉 决定"摄影级 vs 廉价渲染感"

### 5️⃣ 色温与色彩关系（Color Temperature）

* (warm light: golden tone)
* (cool light: bluish tint)

* (color separation between subject and background)
* (skin tone preserved under lighting)

👉 情绪控制核心参数
