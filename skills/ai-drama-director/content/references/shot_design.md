# Shot Design Reference

## Shot Size Definitions

| Shot Size | English | Usage |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| Extreme Wide Shot (EWS) | Large establishing shot | Show vast scene, establish environment |
| Wide Shot (WS) | Full shot | Relationship between character and background |
| Medium Wide Shot (MWS) | Narrative shot | Storytelling scenes |
| Medium Shot (MS) | Knee shot | From waist up |
| Medium Close-Up (MCU) | Cowboy shot | From waist up (US naming) |
| Close-Up (CU) | Bust shot | From shoulders up |
| Extreme Close-Up (ECU) | Detail shot | Partial details like eyes, props |

## Camera Angle Definitions

| Angle | Usage |
|-------|-------|
| Eye Level | Objective, neutral |
| Low Angle | Subject appears tall/commanding |
| High Angle | Subject appears small/vulnerable |
| Bird's Eye | Top-down overview |
| Dutch Angle | Tilted, tension/anxiety |

## Camera Movements

- **Push In**: Gradually approach subject
- **Pull Out**: Gradually move away from subject
- **Pan**: Horizontal rotation
- **Follow**: Track subject's movement
- **Static**: Stationary shot

## Shot Duration Reference

- Short video platforms: 2-5 sec/shot
- Formal short drama: 3-8 sec/shot
- Slow-paced scenes: 5-15 sec/shot
