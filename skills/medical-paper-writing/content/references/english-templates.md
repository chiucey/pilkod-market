# 医学论文英文句型模板库

## 引言部分 (Introduction)

### 描述知识空白

- "However, the relationship between X and Y remains **unclear**."
- "Limited evidence exists regarding the effectiveness of X in [population]."
- "Despite extensive research on X, **gaps remain** in our understanding of..."
- "To date, no study has investigated the effect of X on Y in [specific population]."

### 强调研究必要性

- "Therefore, there is an urgent need for..."
- "Given this gap in knowledge, [intervention/study] is warranted."
- "A better understanding of the mechanisms underlying X is **essential** for..."
- "These findings highlight the importance of..."

### 研究目的陈述

- "This study aimed to investigate/evaluate/examine..."
- "We conducted a [study type] to assess the effect of X on Y in..."
- "The primary objective was to determine whether..."
- "Here, we sought to explore the association between X and Y..."

## 方法部分 (Methods)

### 研究设计描述

- "This was a **prospective/retrospective** cohort study conducted at..."
- "Participants were recruited from [setting] between [dates]."
- "Eligible participants were aged [range] and had..."
- "Patients were **randomly assigned** (1:1) to receive X or Y using..."

### 干预措施描述

- "Participants in the intervention group received [description], whereas..."
- "The treatment protocol consisted of [dose, frequency, duration]."
- "[Procedure] was performed according to standard clinical guidelines."
- "Adherence to the intervention was assessed by..."

### 结局指标定义

- "The primary outcome was [defined clearly]."
- "Secondary outcomes included [list]."
- "Outcomes were assessed at [time points] using [instrument/method]."
- "Clinical improvement was defined as [specific criteria]."

### 统计分析

- "Continuous variables are presented as **mean ± SD** or **median (IQR)**..."
- "Between-group comparisons were performed using **t-test/Mann-Whitney U test**..."
- "Multivariable **linear/logistic regression** was performed to identify factors associated with..."
- "Survival analysis was conducted using **Kaplan-Meier** methods and **Cox regression**..."
- "All analyses were performed using [software] version [X]."
- "A two-sided P value < 0.05 was considered statistically significant."

## 结果部分 (Results)

### 基线特征描述

- "A total of [N] participants were enrolled, with [n] in the X group and [n] in the Y group."
- "Baseline characteristics were **well balanced** between groups."
- "The mean age was [X ± SD] years, and [X%] were male."
- "There were no significant differences in baseline characteristics between groups (all P > 0.05)."

### 主要发现呈现

- "After [follow-up period], [intervention] resulted in a **significant reduction** in..."
- "The intervention group showed [X%] improvement compared with [Y%] in the control group (P = 0.XX)."
- "Mean [outcome] decreased from [baseline] to [follow-up] in the intervention group..."
- "No significant difference was observed in [outcome] between groups (mean difference: X, 95% CI: X to Y)."

### 亚组分析

- "In **prespecified** subgroup analyses, the effect was more pronounced in..."
- "Subgroup analysis by [factor] revealed a significant interaction (P for interaction = 0.XX)."
- "The treatment effect was **consistent** across prespecified subgroups."

### 不良事件

- "Adverse events occurred in [X%] of participants in the intervention group vs. [Y%] in the control group."
- "No serious adverse events were reported in either group."
- "The most common adverse event was [description], occurring in [X%] of participants."

## 讨论部分 (Discussion)

### 结果解释

- "Our findings demonstrate that..."
- "This study shows for the first time that..."
- "The observed [effect/increase/decrease] may be explained by..."
- "These results are consistent with the hypothesis that..."

### 与既往研究比较

- "Our findings are consistent with previous studies reporting..."
- "In contrast to [author], we found that..."
- "This observation differs from earlier reports by [author] et al., who..."
- "Compared with [landmark study], our results show [similar/different] trends."

### 临床意义

- "These findings have important **implications** for clinical practice."
- "Our results suggest that [intervention] should be considered as a **first-line treatment** for..."
- "Healthcare providers should be aware of the potential risks and benefits..."
- "This evidence supports the inclusion of X in clinical guidelines."

### 研究局限性

- "Several limitations should be acknowledged."
- "First, the **observational design** precludes causal inference."
- "Second, [specific limitation related to sample/population/methods]."
- "Third, the relatively **short follow-up period** may not capture long-term outcomes."
- "Despite these limitations, our study has several strengths, including..."

### 结论

- "In conclusion, [restate main finding]."
- "Our data suggest that..."
- "Further studies are needed to confirm these findings and explore..."
- "Long-term randomized controlled trials are warranted to..."

## 常用连接词

- Furthermore / Moreover / Additionally
- In addition / Besides / Also

### 对比
- However / Nevertheless / Nonetheless
- In contrast / Conversely / On the contrary

### 因果
- Therefore / Thus / Hence / Consequently
- As a result / Accordingly / Because of this

### 举例
- For example / For instance / Such as
- Specifically / In particular / Notably

### 强调
- Importantly / Significantly / Notably
- Indeed / In fact / Actually

### 总结
- In summary / To summarize / Overall
- In brief / In short / To conclude
