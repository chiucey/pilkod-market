# The Art of War - Strategic Wisdom Skill

## Overview

This skill provides strategic guidance based on Sun Tzu's ancient military treatise "The Art of War" (孙子兵法). It applies timeless principles of strategy, leadership, and tactics to modern contexts including business, negotiations, competition, and personal challenges.

## Core Principles

### 1. Laying Plans (始计)
- **Know yourself and your enemy**: Victory requires understanding both your strengths and the opponent's weaknesses
- **Five constant factors**: Moral Law, Heaven, Earth, The Commander, Method and discipline
- **All warfare is based on deception**: Appear weak when strong, strong when weak

### 2. Waging War (作战)
- **Speed is key**: Quick victories prevent exhaustion of resources
- **Prolonged war depletes**: Extended campaigns drain strength and invite other threats
- **Forage on the enemy**: Use opponent's resources to sustain your forces

### 3. Attack by Stratagem (谋攻)
- **Supreme excellence**: Break enemy's resistance without fighting
- **Hierarchy of victory**:
  1. Balk enemy's plans
  2. Prevent enemy forces from joining
  3. Attack enemy in the field
  4. Besiege walled cities (worst option)
- **Know when to fight**: Victory requires knowing when to engage and when to avoid battle

### 4. Tactical Dispositions (形)
- **Secure against defeat first**: Make yourself invulnerable, then wait for victory
- **Win by making no mistakes**: Perfection comes from exploiting enemy errors
- **Control success**: Through moral law and strict discipline

### 5. Energy (势)
- **Direct and indirect methods**: Combine both for endless tactical possibilities
- **Like water**: Tactics adapt to circumstances, flowing around obstacles
- **Combined energy**: Unify forces for devastating impact

### 6. Weak Points and Strong (虚实)
- **Impose your will**: Don't let the enemy impose theirs on you
- **Attack weakness, avoid strength**: Hit where the enemy is vulnerable
- **Conceal your dispositions**: Keep plans secret until execution

### 7. Maneuvering (军争)
- **Turn devious into direct**: Use indirect approaches to gain advantage
- **Rapid as wind, immovable as mountain**: Speed when attacking, steadfast when defending
- **Plans dark as night**: Move like thunderbolt when ready

### 8. Variation in Tactics (九变)
- **No constant conditions**: Adapt methods to infinite variety of circumstances
- **Flexibility is paramount**: Be ready to change approach based on developments

### 9. The Nine Situations (九地)
- **Different terrain, different tactics**: Adjust strategy based on ground
- **散地**: Don't concentrate
- **轻地**: Don't stop
- **争地**: Don't attack prematurely
- **交地**: Don't allow separation
- **衢地**: Form alliances
- **重地**: Seize resources
- **圮地**: Keep moving
- **围地**: Find way out
- **死地**: Fight desperately

### 10. The Army on the March (行军)
- **Discipline and order**: Maintain control in all circumstances
- **Care for soldiers**: Lead with benevolence while maintaining strictness

### 11. Terrain (地形)
- **Six types of terrain**: Each requires different approach
- **Know the ground**: Essential for tactical advantage

### 12. The Attack by Fire (火攻)
- **Use environment**: Fire as force multiplier
- **Timing and conditions**: Fire attacks require specific circumstances

### 13. The Use of Spies (用间)
- **Knowledge is power**: Spies provide critical intelligence
- **Five types of spies**: Local, inward, convert, doomed, living
- **Greatest generals**: Use spies extensively

## Application Framework

When applying these principles, follow this framework:

1. **Assessment**: Evaluate all five constant factors
2. **Comparison**: Determine advantages in seven key areas
3. **Strategy**: Plan based on strength and weakness analysis
4. **Deception**: Control information and appearances
5. **Execution**: Move decisively when opportunity arises
6. **Adaptation**: Modify plans based on circumstances

## Key Quotes

- "Know yourself and your enemy, and you will win a hundred battles"
- "The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting"
- "In war, let your great object be victory, not lengthy campaigns"
- "All warfare is based on deception"
- "He who knows when to fight and when not to fight will be victorious"
