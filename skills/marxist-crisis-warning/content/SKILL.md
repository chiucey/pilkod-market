# Marxist Political Economy Crisis Warning Skill

## Core Analysis Framework

### I. Theoretical Foundation

## II. Four-Dimensional Core Indicator System

### Indicator I: Profit Rate (Core Crisis Indicator)

**Monitoring Logic**: Sustained profit rate decline → capital flees to financial speculation → bubble accumulation → crisis eruption

**Search Indicators**:
- US non-financial corporate profit rate (pre-tax/after-tax)
- S&P 500 component average net profit margin
- Global major economies manufacturing profit rate

**Alert Threshold**: 4-6 consecutive quarters of trending decline, with significant widening gap vs financial asset returns

### Indicator II: Real Interest Rate (Credit Cycle Pulse)

**Monitoring Logic**: Real interest rate shifts from negative to positive and climbs → financing costs rise → debt burden increases → credit chain break risk

**Search Indicators**:
- 10-Year US Treasury real yield (TIPS)
- Federal funds rate minus inflation expectations
- High-yield bond credit spread

**Alert Threshold**: 10-Year TIPS breaks through 2% for 3+ months, credit spread expands to 600bp+

### Indicator III: Foreign Exchange Reserves (Balance of Payments Pressure)

**Monitoring Logic**: Rapid reserve depletion → capital flight → currency crisis warning

**Search Indicators**:
- Emerging market aggregate foreign reserve change rate
- China monthly foreign reserve changes
- India and other countries' external debt/reserve ratio

**Alert Threshold**: 15%+ decline within 6 months, external debt/reserve ratio exceeds 200%

### Indicator IV: Real Exchange Rate (Competitiveness & Bubble Mirror)

**Monitoring Logic**: Persistent real exchange rate overvaluation → export competitiveness declines → current account deteriorates → external vulnerability rises

**Search Indicators**:
- US Real Effective Exchange Rate (REER)
- China Real Effective Exchange Rate
- Current account balance/GDP ratio

**Alert Threshold**: USD REER breaks historical mean 1.5 standard deviations, current account deficit/GDP exceeds 5%
