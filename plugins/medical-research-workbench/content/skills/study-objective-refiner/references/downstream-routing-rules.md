# Downstream Routing Rules

After refining the objective, recommend the best next-step tool or workflow.

## Route to Primary Plan Recommender
Use `primary-plan-recommender` when the objective is bounded enough to compare study-route families
and select a main plan.

## Route to Feasibility-Aware Study Planner
Use `feasibility-aware-study-planner` when the objective is concrete but must be redesigned around
real samples, time, budget, data access, laboratory capacity, team skills, or validation resources.

## Route to Evidence Mapping and Gap Finding
Use `topic-evidence-mapper` when the objective still depends on evidence positioning. Use
`medical-research-gap-finder` for formal gap confirmation only with verified retrieval or adequate
user-supplied sources.

## Route to Methods or Validation Review
Use `methods-reverse-engineer` when a source workflow needs reconstruction, or
`validation-strategy-designer` when the unresolved need is validation architecture.

## Rule
Do not leave the user with a refined objective but no recommended next step.
